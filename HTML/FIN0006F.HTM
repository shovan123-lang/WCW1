THE ROAD TO BALTIMORE: PILLMAN VS. LIGER, AND MR. WONDERFUL ON JYD
Greenville, Memorial Auditorium | Segment 5 (3:00)

A video package rolls, with music under it. Footage from Philadelphia:
Brian Pillman and Jushin Liger trading high-flying moves, then Pillman
catching Liger in the crucifix for the three-count.

JIM ROSS (voiceover): Philadelphia, July 5th. Brian Pillman and Jushin
Liger tore the house down, and Pillman got the win with a crucifix Liger
never saw coming.

Footage from Atlanta: Liger walks over to Pillman and offers his hand.
Pillman looks at it for a moment, then shakes it.

JIM ROSS (voiceover): The next night in Atlanta, Liger asked for one more
chance, and Pillman shook on it. Since then, Liger hasn't lost. Pillman
has been at war with Bobby Eaton and Paul E. Dangerously. Tomorrow
night, the rematch. Pillman. Liger. The Great American Bash.

The package ends. Cut to Paul Orndorff in front of a WCW backdrop, arms
folded, a towel around his neck.

PAUL ORNDORFF: This morning on WCW Pro, Terry Taylor found out what
happens when you get in the ring with Mr. Wonderful. Piledriver. One,
two, three. That's one step closer to the Television Title. But before
I worry about that, I've got a little business in Baltimore.

PAUL ORNDORFF: Junkyard Dog. I hear you've been telling people you're
going to teach me some manners. Dog, I've been in main events all over
this country. I've been in there with the very best in the world. And
I've watched you dance with the kids at ringside for ten years. That's
cute. Tomorrow night you can bark all you want. When I'm through, the
Dog is going to be lying in the middle of that ring, and Baltimore is
going to find out what Paul Orndorff is all about. Wonderful.

Orndorff smirks, drops the towel and walks out of frame.
