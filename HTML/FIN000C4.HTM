BARRY WINDHAM RESPONDS
Johnson City, TN | Segment 4 (2:00)

The interview area. Eric Bischoff. Barry Windham in jeans and a WCW
T-shirt.

ERIC BISCHOFF: Barry, you just heard Rick Rude.

BARRY WINDHAM: I heard him. Two weeks ago I was in a cage at the Great
American Bash fighting for the World Title. Since then, Nikita got a
title shot and walked out with the belt. Ron Simmons is getting his
chances. And I've been standing in the back, watching.

I'm done watching. Sting's gone, and I'm going to take what's mine.
Every town Sting was booked in, Barry Windham is coming instead.

He points into the camera.

BARRY WINDHAM: And Rude, I want the United States Title. Not when you
get back from Japan. Not when it's convenient for Paul E. Now.

ERIC BISCHOFF: Rude has Bobby Eaton and Paul E. in his corner.

BARRY WINDHAM: Then I'll bring a friend too. Rick Steiner hasn't
forgotten the Bash, when Rude beat him with Paul E. hanging on his leg.
So here's what happens. Tuesday night, Inglewood, California. Barry
Windham and Rick Steiner against Rick Rude and Bobby Eaton. Bring the
phone, Paul E. Bring whatever you want.

Windham walks off.
