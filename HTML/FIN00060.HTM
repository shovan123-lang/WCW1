WCW PRO: OPEN AND THE TV TITLE TOURNAMENT
Atlanta, WCW Pro Studio | Segment 1 (2:00)

The WCW Pro open rolls. Cut to the desk, where Tony Schiavone and Jim
Ross sit in front of the WCW logo. A large bracket board with eight blank
slots stands between them.

TONY SCHIAVONE: Welcome to WCW Pro! I'm Tony Schiavone, alongside Jim
Ross, and we are less than thirty-six hours from the Great American Bash
in Baltimore. But before we get to Baltimore, Jim, we have breaking news
of our own.

JIM ROSS: We do, Tony. The WCW Television Championship is vacant, and the
WCW office has decided that it will not simply be handed to somebody. It
will be won. Starting today, right here on WCW Pro, eight of the best
wrestlers in this company will compete in a single-elimination tournament
for the Television Title.

The board lights up as Schiavone reads each name. The bracket graphic
fills the screen.

TONY SCHIAVONE: In the top half of the bracket, Paul Orndorff against
Terry Taylor, and Larry Zbyszko against Robert Gibson. In the bottom half,
Rick Steiner against Arn Anderson, and Tommy Rich against Bobby Eaton.
Every match is under Television Title rules, fifteen-minute time limit,
and the final will be held right here on WCW Pro three weeks from today.

JIM ROSS: And we start with two quarterfinals today. Mr. Wonderful Paul
Orndorff, who has been on a tear this summer, against Terry Taylor. And
in our main event, the Dog-Faced Gremlin Rick Steiner against the
Enforcer, Arn Anderson. Two men who'd rather fight than eat.

TONY SCHIAVONE: Plus Barry Windham, one night before he steps into the
steel cage with Lex Luger, is in action right now. Let's go to ringside.
