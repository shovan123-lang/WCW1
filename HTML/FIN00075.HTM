FINAL WORDS BEFORE THE CAGE: LEX LUGER AND BARRY WINDHAM
Greenville, Memorial Auditorium | Segment 11 (3:00)

Tony Schiavone stands on the interview set with Lex Luger, who is in his
ring jacket with his arms crossed. Harley Race and Mr. Hughes wait off to
the side.

TONY SCHIAVONE: Lex, twenty-four hours from now, you and Barry Windham
are locked in a steel cage with the World Title hanging over it all.

LEX LUGER: Tony, I've been the uncrowned champion of this company for
three years. Three years of being told I'm next. Three years of watching
somebody else walk out with the belt. Tomorrow night, nobody is going to
tell me I'm next. Tomorrow night, the Total Package becomes the World
Heavyweight Champion, and there's nothing Barry Windham can do about it.

LEX LUGER: Barry, you picked the cage. You wanted to make sure I couldn't
get out. Well, I've got news for you, pal. I'm not going anywhere. I'm
going to be in there with you until it's over, and when it's over,
you're going to be lying on that mat looking up at the new champion.

Luger turns and walks off with Race and Hughes. Cut to the back, where
Jim Ross is standing with Barry Windham in front of the locker room door.

JIM ROSS: Barry, you heard him. Your last words before the cage.

BARRY WINDHAM: Jim, my daddy used to tell me a cage doesn't lie. You can
talk all you want outside of it, but once that door is locked, the man
who walks out is the better man. Lex Luger talks a lot about the last
three years. I want to talk about tomorrow night.

BARRY WINDHAM: Lex, you've got Harley Race, and you've got Mr. Hughes. And
when that door locks, you won't have either one of them. You'll have me.
I'm going to take you to the top of that cage if I have to, and I'm
going to bring you down the hard way. Tomorrow night, a Windham walks out
of Baltimore as the World Heavyweight Champion.

Windham pushes through the locker room door, and it slams shut behind
him.
