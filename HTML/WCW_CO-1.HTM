Match observations: 

WCW MAGAZINE: ROAD TO THE BASH
Columbia, South Carolina | Carolina Coliseum | July 11, 1991

WINDHAM SNAPS! CHAIR SHOT COSTS BARRY IN COLUMBIA

Three nights before he steps into the steel cage with Lex Luger, Barry
Windham showed 5,200 fans at the Carolina Coliseum just how close to the
edge he is.

For more than twenty minutes, Windham and U.S. Champion Sting went toe to
toe with Luger and Mr. Hughes, with Harley Race barking orders from the
floor. Then Hughes did what everyone feared he would. He slid a steel chair
into the ring. This time Windham got to it first. He tore it out of the big
man's hands and wrapped it around him in full view of the referee, who had
no choice but to call for the bell. Luger and Hughes were awarded the win
by disqualification.

Sting had to pull his partner off Hughes before Windham could do more
damage. As he left the ring, Windham pointed at Luger and shouted, "Sunday
there's no referee to throw me out!" He's right. In Baltimore, it's the
two of them locked inside a steel cage, with the WCW World Title hanging
in the balance.

RACE TAKES AIM AT KOLOFF

Harley Race has his hands full managing Lex Luger, but he still found time
to settle a score. In the semi-main, Rick Steiner and Nikita Koloff were
battling Big Van Vader and the Great Muta when Race came down the aisle and
grabbed Koloff by the boot. Vader took full advantage and crushed the
Russian Nightmare with the Vader Bomb at 18:41. Is Race sending a message
to the man who wants Luger's rematch? Koloff will have to put it out of
his mind fast. He challenges Sting for the U.S. Title on Sunday.

EATON CASHES IN

Bobby Eaton keeps paying dividends for Paul E. Dangerously. With Paul E.
watching from ringside, Beautiful Bobby turned back Brian Pillman in a
fast-paced 15:49 battle and finished him off with the Alabama Jam.
Pillman will have to regroup in a hurry, with Jushin Liger waiting in
Baltimore.

DOOM VS. DOOM

Ron Simmons and Butch Reed used to be the most feared tag team in WCW.
Tonight they were on opposite sides of the ring, and Simmons proved he's
the better man, ending a hard-hitting fight with the spinebuster. Simmons
heads to the Bash for his showdown with Ozz.

AROUND THE RING

Jushin Liger stopped Diamond Dallas Page with the Liger Bomb. The
Fabulous Freebirds kept rolling as Michael Hayes dropped Tommy Rich with
the DDT. Arn Anderson took apart Robert Gibson. Richard Morton cradled
Tracy Smothers to give the York Foundation the win in the opener. And El
Gigante needed only four minutes to chokeslam Bill Irwin.
