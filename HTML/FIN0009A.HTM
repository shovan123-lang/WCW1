INTERVIEW: LEX LUGER WITH HARLEY RACE & MR. HUGHES

Tony Schiavone stands on the interview set. Lex Luger walks out with the
World Title over his shoulder, Harley Race on one side and Mr. Hughes on
the other. Race is still breathing hard from his trip to ringside during
the Vader match.

Tony asks Luger how it feels to be champion. Luger takes the belt off his
shoulder and holds it up to the camera. He says he spent years hearing he
couldn't win the big one, and in Baltimore he won it inside a cage where
nobody could help him. Now he's the man, and the man doesn't do things the
way he used to.

Tony brings up Race and Hughes. Luger says a champion protects what's his.
Harley Race has held this title more times than anybody in the building,
and he knows what it takes to keep it.

Race steps in and points at the camera. "Barry Windham. Ron Simmons. Every
one of you lining up out there. I've been around this business a long
time and I know what you're all thinking. Here's how it works now. Nobody
gets in, nobody gets out. Not without going through us." Hughes says
nothing and just stares at the camera.

Luger closes: "Barry, you had your shot in the cage and you came up short.
Tonight you get Mr. Hughes. Enjoy it." The three of them walk off.
