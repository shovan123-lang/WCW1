ARN ANDERSON & LARRY ZBYSZKO CONFRONTATION
Atlanta, GA, Center Stage | Segment 19 (1:20:00–1:24:00)

Cut to the interview area. Eric Bischoff stands with Arn Anderson, who is dressed in
a sharp suit, arms folded, calm and serious.

ERIC BISCHOFF: Arn Anderson, two nights ago in Philadelphia you went almost twenty
minutes with the Junk Yard Dog before finishing him with the DDT. It was a war.

ARN ANDERSON: It was a war, Eric, and I respect the Dog. He hit me as hard as
anybody's hit me in a long time. But when the dust settled, the Enforcer did what
the Enforcer always does. I found an opening, I took it, and I finished the job.
That's not luck. That's twenty years of knowing how to win a fight.

A voice interrupts from off camera.

LARRY ZBYSZKO: Twenty years of knowing how to get lucky, you mean.

Larry Zbyszko strolls into frame in a flashy jacket, smirking. The crowd boos.

LARRY ZBYSZKO: Bischoff, I watched that match. The Dog had Anderson out on his feet.
Twice. If JYD had one more breath in him, Arn would've been carried out of that
building on a stretcher. Instead the Dog slipped, and Arn fell into a DDT. That's
not a win, Arn. That's a fluke.

ARN ANDERSON: (slowly turning to face him) A fluke.

LARRY ZBYSZKO: You heard me. Everybody talks about Arn Anderson like he's some kind
of legend. Well, there's only one living legend in WCW, and he's standing right
in front of you.

ARN ANDERSON: Larry, I've known you a long time. And in all that time, you've
always had one problem. You talk like a champion, and you wrestle like a man
who's afraid to get his hair messed up.

(The crowd laughs. Zbyszko's smile disappears.)

ARN ANDERSON: You want to call what I did a fluke? That's fine. But do it where it
counts. The Great American Bash. You and me. No partners, no excuses. And when I
plant you with that DDT, you can tell everybody in Baltimore how lucky I got.

LARRY ZBYSZKO: (stepping into his face) You're on, Anderson. And when I'm done with
you, they'll be calling YOU the fluke.

Zbyszko shoves Arn. Arn answers with a right hand that sends Zbyszko staggering
back into the WCW backdrop. Zbyszko charges, and the two of them crash to the
floor, trading punches. Bischoff jumps out of the way.

ERIC BISCHOFF: Somebody get back here! Get some help!

Officials and agents rush into the frame and try to pull them apart. Arn breaks
free and lands one more shot before he's dragged away. Zbyszko is held back by
three officials, screaming at Arn as he goes.

LARRY ZBYSZKO: Baltimore, Anderson! BALTIMORE!

Cut back to the desk.

TONY SCHIAVONE: JR, those two have been circling each other for weeks, and it
finally boiled over.

JIM ROSS: Arn Anderson and Larry Zbyszko, two of the smartest men in this
business, and neither one of them is smart enough to walk away. That's gonna be a
clinic in Baltimore, Tony, and it might turn into a street fight.

Cut to break.
