WCW SATURDAY NIGHT RECAP: A NEW ERA BEGINS
Atlanta, WCW Pro Studio | Segment 3 (3:00)

Back at the desk. Schiavone and Ross sit with the Saturday Night logo on
the monitor behind them.

TONY SCHIAVONE: Welcome back to WCW Pro. Last Saturday night in Atlanta,
WCW Saturday Night was the first show of a new era, and if you missed it,
here's what happened.

Roll footage: Jim Herd at the podium at Center Stage.

JIM ROSS (voiceover): Jim Herd, the WCW executive vice president, opened
the show and addressed the situation head on. Ric Flair is no longer
with World Championship Wrestling, and the WCW World Heavyweight
Championship belt left with him. Herd unveiled a brand-new World Title
belt and announced that it will be decided at the Great American Bash,
inside a steel cage, between Lex Luger and Barry Windham.

Footage: the new belt on a pedestal, then Luger and Harley Race smirking
from the aisle.

TONY SCHIAVONE (voiceover): Later in the night, the Rock 'n' Roll Express
beat Bobby Eaton and Diamond Dallas Page, and they celebrated the way they
have for years. Then Richard Morton turned on Robert Gibson.

Footage: Morton blasting Gibson from behind, then grabbing the microphone.

TONY SCHIAVONE (voiceover): "My name is RICHARD Morton." Eight years as a
team, and it ended in one night. Morton and Gibson meet at the Bash.

JIM ROSS (voiceover): And in the main event, Barry Windham took out Ozz
with the superplex. Then he called out Lex Luger, and Luger answered from
the stage with Harley Race and Mr. Hughes beside him. Ron Simmons came
out to stand with Windham, and that's where we left it.

Back to the desk.

JIM ROSS: Also last Saturday: the Bruise Brothers debuted and made a mess
of Bill Irwin and Butch Reed, Brian Pillman and Jushin Liger shook hands
on a rematch at the Bash, and Arn Anderson and Larry Zbyszko nearly came
to blows before their Bash match was even signed. Tony, it was a lot.

TONY SCHIAVONE: And it's only getting bigger. When we come back, the
Fabulous Freebirds are in action.
