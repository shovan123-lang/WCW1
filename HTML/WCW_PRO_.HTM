Match observations: 

WCW Pro Tapings| July 3rd, 1991
East Rutherford, NJ |Venue:Meadowlands
Attendance: 6,000 |AVG Ticket Price: $15.00 
Gate $90,000| Broadcast: TBS Superstation

Big news coming out of the Pro tapings is that a TV title tournament
will be taking place to crown the new champion. This tourament will take
place starting on the WCW Saturday Night shows taped on July 6th. Participant
names were not revealed. 

We saw plenty of the WCW stars in action this week with PN News, The
Fabulous Freebirds, The Killer Bees and Cactus Jack picking up impressive
victories. 

Some of the featured matches had Brian Pillman taking on the high flying
Jushin Liger. Pillman made reference to the TV title and looks to be
eager to get a shot at the title. 

Lex Luger and Mr Hughes brawled all over the ring with Lex securing the
countout victory. 

Doom and the Young Pistols went head to head. Ron and Butch Reed came away
with the win in that exciting match.

Vader made short work of Terry Taylor, and Abdullah scored a win in the
10 man battle royal. 

These matches will air on TBS in the coming weeks.
