WCW SATURDAY NIGHT: OPEN
Johnson City, TN | Segment 1 (4:00)

Live on TBS. Jim Ross and Tony Schiavone at the desk. The tag team
tournament bracket is on the screen.

JIM ROSS: Welcome to WCW Saturday Night, live from Johnson City,
Tennessee! Tonight, for the first time, the WCW World Tag Team
Championship will be decided. Four teams, three matches, one set of
belts, and it all happens tonight.

TONY SCHIAVONE: The Fabulous Freebirds face the Bruise Brothers. The
Young Pistols face the York Foundation. The winners meet in our main
event. But JR, before any of that...

Music. Nikita Koloff walks out with the World Title over his shoulder.
He stops at the desk and takes a headset.

JIM ROSS: The new World Heavyweight Champion. Nikita, two nights ago in
Cleveland you beat Lex Luger. Last night in Charleston, Harley Race and
Mr. Hughes did everything they could to take it back.

NIKITA KOLOFF: They tried. They failed. The belt is still here.

But I came to say something I have not said to anyone. My neck. You
have seen me hold it after every match. The doctors say it will not
hold up much longer. So this is my last ride, Jim. And when it is over,
I walk out of this business as the World Champion.

TONY SCHIAVONE: Nikita, are you telling us you're retiring?

NIKITA KOLOFF: I am telling you I will not lose this belt to anyone.
Then I go home.

Music. Lex Luger and Harley Race walk onto the stage. Luger has a mic.

LEX LUGER: Last ride? Nikita, I'll make it easy for you. You don't have
to decide when you retire. I'll be the man who retires you.

HARLEY RACE: And the contract says Lex Luger gets his rematch. Every
town. Every night. Nashville Monday, Inglewood Tuesday, and everywhere
after that. Nobody gets in, nobody gets out, Russian.

Koloff takes off the headset, holds the belt up toward the stage and
nods. Luger and Race back through the curtain.

JIM ROSS: Every night, Tony. Every single night.
