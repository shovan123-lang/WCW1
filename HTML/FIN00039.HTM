WCW SATURDAY NIGHT: LOCAL PROMOS & ANNOUNCEMENTS

Atlanta, GA, Center Stage | Segment 21
(1:35:00–1:38:00)



Cut to the WCW broadcast desk. Jim Ross sits alone in a headset with a stack of 
cue cards. Behind him is a backdrop of WCW event posters.



JIM ROSS: Welcome back to WCW Saturday Night. Folks, the Great American Bash tour 
doesn’t slow down, and neither do we. Coming up in our main event, it’s 
Barry Windham against Ozz. But first, WCW is coming to a town near you. 
Let’s start in the Old Dominion. Richmond, Virginia, this one’s for you.



Cut to a pre-taped insert in front of a blue WCW backdrop with “RICHMOND COLISEUM, 
SUN. JULY 7” across the bottom of the screen. Sting and Barry Windham stand 
shoulder to shoulder.



STING: Richmond! Tomorrow night, the Stinger and Barry Windham are coming to the 
Coliseum, and we’re bringing a whole lot of trouble for Lex Luger and Mr. Hughes!



WINDHAM: Luger, you’ve got Harley Race in your corner and Hughes watching your back.
Fine. Bring ’em. Tomorrow night in Richmond, I’m gonna get a little preview of what
I’m gonna do to you inside that cage in Baltimore.



STING: (grinning) Richmond! WHOOOOO!



Cut to a second Richmond insert. 
Paul Orndorff stands alone, polishing his sunglasses.


ORNDORFF: Richmond, Virginia. You saw what I did to Rick Steiner in Philadelphia.
Tomorrow night, Tommy Rich, you get the same treatment. And for all you lucky 
ladies in the Coliseum… you get to see Mr. Wonderful in person. 
(He puts the sunglasses on.) You’re welcome.



Cut to a third Richmond insert. 
Brian Pillman is bouncing on his toes, full of energy.



PILLMAN: Bobby Eaton, they call you “Beautiful,” and you’re one of the best to 
ever lace ’em up. But I just went twenty minutes with the greatest junior 
heavyweight in the world, and I’m flyin’ higher than ever. 
Richmond, tomorrow night, Flyin’ Brian is takin’ off! 



Back to Jim Ross at the desk. 

JIM ROSS: And there’s more in Richmond: 
the Bruise Brothers take on the Killer Bees, plus El Gigante, Arn Anderson 
and “Ravishing” Rick Rude, all at the Richmond Coliseum, tomorrow night.

Tickets are still available at the box office.


He flips to the next card.



JIM ROSS: And on Monday, we head to Columbus, Georgia, and folks, this one is 
special. The Fabulous Freebirds are back together! Michael Hayes returns to join 
Jimmy Garvin. Plus, Big Van Vader and the Great Muta take on Rick Steiner and Nikita
Koloff, two men who are looking for redemption after Philadelphia. And 
Jushin “Thunder” Liger goes one-on-one with Dustin Rhodes. Sting, Arn Anderson, 
Larry Zbyszko and Kevin Sullivan’s Dark Circle will all be at the Columbus Civic 
Center, Monday night.



A full-screen graphic rolls through the upcoming dates as JR reads them.



JIM ROSS: On Tuesday, we’re in Tallahassee at the Leon County Civic Center for 
WCW Pro. 

Later this month we head west to the Oakland Coliseum on July 26th. But all roads 
lead to one place.

The graphic changes to a dramatic GREAT AMERICAN BASH ‘91 logo 
over a steel cage.



JIM ROSS: Sunday, July 14th. The Baltimore Arena. The Great American Bash. 
Lex Luger. Barry Windham. One steel cage. One vacant World Heavyweight Championship.
Plus Sting against Nikita Koloff, Pillman and Liger part two, Rude against Steiner,
and much, much more. It’s the biggest night of the summer, and you do not want to 
miss it.



JR sets down his cards and looks into the camera.



JIM ROSS: Business is about to pick up, folks. Don’t go anywhere. 
Barry Windham and Ozz are next!



Cut to break.
