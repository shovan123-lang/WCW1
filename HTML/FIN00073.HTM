ONE LAST LOOK: STING AND NIKITA KOLOFF
Greenville, Memorial Auditorium | Segment 9 (3:00)

Jim Ross stands in the ring holding a microphone. Sting walks out to a
huge reaction, the United States Title on his shoulder. Then Nikita
Koloff's music hits, and Koloff walks down the aisle slowly, staring at
the belt the whole way. He climbs in. They stand a few feet apart.

JIM ROSS: Gentlemen, tomorrow night, the United States Championship.
Nikita, you go first.

NIKITA KOLOFF: Jim, I came back to World Championship Wrestling for one
reason. Not money. Not to make friends. Gold. I've wrestled the
best in the world, and I respect this man. I have stood beside him and I
have stood across from him. But respect does not win titles. Tomorrow
night in Baltimore, I take that belt home.

The crowd boos a little. Sting smiles and takes the microphone.

STING: Nikita, you've never been anything but straight with me, so I'll
be straight with you. I'm not going to hold anything back tomorrow
night, and I know you won't either. This belt means everything to me,
and it means everything to these people. If you want it, you're going
to have to beat the Stinger at his very best. And I'll be at my best.

Sting holds out his hand. Koloff looks at it for a long moment, then
takes it. Neither man lets go. They squeeze, harder and harder, staring
at each other, until Jim Ross steps between them.

JIM ROSS: Gentlemen. Save it for Baltimore.

They finally let go. Sting raises the title over his head. Koloff raises
one finger toward it, then backs out of the ring.
