"RAVISHING" RICK RUDE PRE-TAPED PROMO
Atlanta, GA, Center Stage | Segment 14 (56:00–58:00)

Cut to the broadcast desk.

JIM ROSS: Folks, "Ravishing" Rick Rude isn't with us here in Atlanta tonight. He's
on tour overseas with All Japan Pro Wrestling. But he sent us a message, and he
asked that it be played for Sting.

Cut to pre-taped footage. The tape is slightly grainy. Rick Rude stands in a
luxury hotel suite at night, in a silk robe, with the neon lights of a Japanese
city glowing through the window behind him. A bottle of champagne sits in an ice
bucket on the table. He takes a slow sip, sets the glass down, and smiles at the
camera.

RICK RUDE: Cut the music.

(He spreads his arms toward the window.)

RICK RUDE: Take a good look, all you fat, out-of-shape sweathogs back in Atlanta.
This is Sendai, Japan. Last night, in front of a sold-out crowd, I beat Johnny Ace.
And tonight I'm sittin' here in a five-star suite, drinkin' the finest champagne in
the Far East, while you're sittin' on your couch in your dirty undershirts eatin'
pork rinds.

(He laughs and turns away from the window.)

RICK RUDE: The people over here understand class. They understand greatness. They
look at Rick Rude and they bow. But I didn't send this tape to talk about Japan.
I sent it to talk to one man.

(He steps closer to the camera. The smile disappears.)

RICK RUDE: Sting. Everywhere I go, all I hear about is Sting. Sting this, Sting
that. The United States Champion. The heart and soul of WCW. Well, let me tell you
something, "Stinger." While you've been runnin' around paintin' your face and
screamin' at children, I've been beatin' the toughest men on the planet on the
other side of the world.

RICK RUDE: First, I've got some unfinished business. Rick Steiner. July 14th, the
Great American Bash. Steiner, you can bark all you want, but in Baltimore I'm
gonna put you to sleep with the Rude Awakening and leave you lyin' in the middle
of the ring.

(He leans in until his face nearly fills the frame.)

RICK RUDE: And then, Sting, I'm coming for you. I'm coming for that United States
Championship. Because a belt like that doesn't belong around the waist of a
cartoon character. It belongs around the waist of the sexiest man alive.

(He straightens up, unties the robe and gives a slow hip swivel.)

RICK RUDE: Sayonara, sweathogs.

He reaches toward the camera and the tape cuts to static.

Cut back to the desk.

TONY SCHIAVONE: JR, he's halfway around the world and that man is still as
arrogant as ever.

JIM ROSS: He is, Tony. But you can't argue with the results. He's winning in
Japan, and in Baltimore he gets Rick Steiner. If Rude wins that one, Sting had
better keep one eye over his shoulder.

TONY SCHIAVONE: When we come back, Kevin Sullivan and the Dark Circle. Don't go
away.
