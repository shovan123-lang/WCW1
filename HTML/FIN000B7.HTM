MURDOCH AND REED JUMP THE KILLER BEES
Tallahassee, FL (taped 7/9) | Segment 3 (3:00)

Taped 7/9. Brian Blair and Jim Brunzell stand at ringside in their
masks with the ring announcer, talking about wanting a shot at the tag
team tournament.

BRIAN BLAIR: Everybody says the Killer Bees are finished. We hear it in
every town. Well, we're still here, we're still flying, and we'll take
on anybody in this company, any time.

JIM BRUNZELL: Anybody.

Dick Murdoch and Butch Reed come over the barricade behind them. Reed
grabs Brunzell and throws him into the ring post. Murdoch spins Blair
around, gets a fistful of the mask and tears it off. He throws it into
the crowd.

Blair covers his face with both hands and drops to his knees. Murdoch
laughs and points at him.

DICK MURDOCH: You wanna hide behind a mask, boy? Not no more!

Murdoch and Reed walk back up the aisle, slapping hands with each other.
Brunzell, his own mask half torn, crawls over and pulls Blair up. Blair
still has his face covered.

JIM ROSS (voiceover): That's low, even for Dick Murdoch. A man's mask
is his pride.

TONY SCHIAVONE (voiceover): Murdoch and Reed have been beaten by the
Bees with a mask switch. Now they've taken the masks away. JR, the Bees
won't forget this.
