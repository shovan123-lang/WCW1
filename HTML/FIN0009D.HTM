ANGLE: PAUL E. PITCHES ARN

Right after the bell, Ron Simmons celebrates his spinebuster win while
Arn Anderson lies flat on the mat. Simmons leaves to a big ovation.

Paul E. Dangerously walks down the aisle, cellular phone in hand, and
slides into the ring. Arn gets to one knee. Paul E. offers him a hand.
Arn stares at it for a long moment, then lets Paul E. help him up.

Paul E. dusts off Arn's shoulder and talks fast into his ear, close
enough that the cameras catch it. "Look at you. The best technician in
this company, flat on his back for a football player. You know why? You
walk out here alone. Alone, you lose." He takes a business card from his
jacket and presses it into Arn's hand. "Three nights ago you couldn't
beat Zbyszko. Tonight you couldn't beat Simmons. Call me."

Paul E. walks out up the aisle. Arn looks at the card, then at Paul E.'s
back. He opens his hand and the card falls to the mat. Arn rolls under
the bottom rope and walks to the back. The card is still lying in the
ring.

JR: "Arn Anderson isn't buying, Tony. At least not tonight."
