THE FREEBIRDS FLY INTO BALTIMORE
Greenville, Memorial Auditorium | Segment 3 (3:00)

Tony Schiavone stands on the interview set. "Badstreet U.S.A." plays, and
Michael Hayes and Jimmy Garvin stroll in with Diamond Dallas Page between
them in a sequined jacket, an unlit cigar in his hand.

TONY SCHIAVONE: Gentlemen, tomorrow night it's the three of you against
the Young Pistols and Big Josh. Michael, you've only been back a week.
Are you ready?

MICHAEL HAYES: Tony, I was born ready, I was raised ready, and I came back
from that hospital bed readier than ever. The Freebirds went away for a
while, and look what happened to this place. Somebody has to put some
style back in World Championship Wrestling.

JIMMY GARVIN: And some class.

DIAMOND DALLAS PAGE: And some cash, baby! Tony, these two are the
greatest tag team that ever lived, and I'm the guy who makes sure the
whole world knows it. Tomorrow night the Young Pistols find out what
happens when two kids with cap guns walk into a real gunfight. And Big
Josh? Josh, buddy, go back to the woods. Find yourself a nice tree.

Hayes and Garvin laugh. The Young Pistols, Tracy Smothers and Steve
Armstrong, walk onto the set, with Big Josh behind them carrying his axe.
Page takes a step back.

TRACY SMOTHERS: Laugh it up, boys. Y'all have been talking about style
and class this whole time, and not one of you mentioned why Big Josh is
in this match. He's in it because Dustin Rhodes is hurt. Dustin would be
standing right here if he could.

STEVE ARMSTRONG: So tomorrow night we're doing it for him. You want to
talk about guns, Page? We're the Young Pistols. We came loaded.

BIG JOSH: Mr. Page, I've chopped down a lot of trees in my life. Never
met one that talked as much as you. Tomorrow night: timber.

Josh plants the head of the axe on the floor at Page's feet. Page jumps
back into Garvin. Hayes pulls his partners off the set, pointing back at
the Pistols the whole way.

TONY SCHIAVONE: That six-man tag could steal the show tomorrow night in
Baltimore.
