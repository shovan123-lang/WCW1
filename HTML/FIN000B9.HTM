DARK CIRCLE: THE CANDLE
Tallahassee, FL (taped 7/9) | Segment 4 (2:00)

Taped 7/9. The arena lights dim around one corner of the ring. Cactus
Jack sits on the apron in the dark, rocking back and forth. He pulls a
short black candle out of his boot, strikes a match on the ring post
and lights it.

CACTUS JACK: This was Kevin's candle. Kevin Sullivan lit it every night
he walked through that curtain. Kevin's gone. Then Mr. Hughes walked out
the door after him. And all of you think the Dark Circle walked out with
them.

He holds the candle right up to the camera.

CACTUS JACK: You're wrong. The flame's still lit. Somebody has to carry
it, and nobody else wanted the job. So now it's mine.

He looks over his shoulder, toward the back.

CACTUS JACK: There are men in that locker room who don't fit anywhere.
Big men. Mean men. Men nobody wants to stand next to. I see you. The
circle is open. Bang bang.

He blows out the candle. Dark.
