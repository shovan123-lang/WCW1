WCW Saturday Night
Johnson City, TN, 7/20/1991
Live on TBS
Attendance: 6,200
Average ticket: $12.00
Gate: $74,400
Merchandise: $12,400

The Fabulous Freebirds are the first WCW World Tag Team Champions.
Michael Hayes and Jimmy Garvin won two matches in one night in Johnson
City, the second with a little help from Diamond Dallas Page, and beat
the Young Pistols in the final in the state the Pistols call home.

The night opened with the new World Heavyweight Champion. Nikita
Koloff came out to the desk with the belt and told Jim Ross and Tony
Schiavone something he hadn't told anyone. His neck is going. The
doctors say it won't hold up much longer, and this is his last ride.
He intends to leave this business as the champion. Lex Luger and
Harley Race answered from the stage. Luger said he'll be the man who
retires Koloff, and Race said Luger's contract guarantees him a
rematch in every town, every night, starting in Nashville Monday and
Inglewood Tuesday.
