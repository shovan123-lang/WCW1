Match observations: 

Great American Bash Tour | July 2nd, 1991
Day 2: Ft. Bragg, North Carolina|Venue:Ritz-Epps Center
Attendance: 850  AVG: Ticket $10 
Gate: $8,500 Broadcast: House Show

Ft. Bragg, home of the US Military played host to the second stop on the
Great American Bash tour. Our main event saw the Fabulous Freebirds take on
The Brass Knuckle Syndicate. With the Tag Titles vacant at the moment, this
victory for the Freebirds puts them in the drivers seat when it comes to
a match for the belts. The same can be said for Doom, and the Young Pistols
who won thier tag matches earlier in the show. 

Sting defeated the Taskmaster Kevin Sullivan one on one, the Stinger worked 
the crowd into a frenzy leading up to a scorpion deathlock that had Sullivan
tapping the mat. 

Cactus Jack and Rick Steiner had a brawl all around the arena, ending in a 
double DQ, while Dustin Rhodes made Richard Morton submit to a figure 4. 
With Ric Flair walking out on WCW, this may not have been the best move to
perform during the match! 

One Man Gang and PN News both earned victories in the opening matches. These
two maybe on a collision corse in the future. 

Tomorrow night we have a big event from the Meadowlands in East Rutherford, NJ.
The event will be a tv taping for WCW Pro, with a few matches taped for
other syndicated shows.
