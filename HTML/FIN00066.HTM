THE DARK CIRCLE: WHERE IS KEVIN SULLIVAN?
Tallahassee, Leon County Civic Center | Segment 7 (3:00)

The arena lights drop to near darkness. A single spotlight finds the
ring, where the Dark Circle's candles have been set out in a circle on
the mat. Mr. Hughes and the One Man Gang walk slowly to the ring. Kevin
Sullivan is not with them.

JIM ROSS: Something isn't right here, Tony. That's Mr. Hughes, that's the
One Man Gang, those are Kevin Sullivan's candles. Where is Sullivan?

TONY SCHIAVONE: Nobody has seen Kevin Sullivan since Columbus, Georgia,
on Monday night. He didn't make the trip to Tallahassee. We've asked, and
nobody in the Dark Circle will say a word.

Hughes and OMG step through the ropes. One by one, OMG lights the
candles with a match. When the last one is lit, they stand on either
side of the circle, waiting. Nothing happens. The crowd starts to
murmur, then to chant Sullivan's name.

JIM ROSS: They're waiting for him. They don't know where he is either.

Hughes looks at OMG, looks at the candles, and shakes his head. He steps
over the circle, ducks through the ropes and walks up the aisle toward
the back without looking back.

TONY SCHIAVONE: Mr. Hughes is leaving! He's walking out on the Dark
Circle!

JIM ROSS: Well, Mr. Hughes has been at ringside for Lex Luger more than
he's been with Sullivan lately, Tony. Maybe he's made his choice.

OMG stands alone in the ring, surrounded by the candles. He kneels and
stares into the flames. The spotlight stays on him as the arena lights
come up around him.

JIM ROSS: The One Man Gang, alone. No Sullivan, no Hughes. I've got a
feeling this circle isn't finished. I just don't know who's going to
close it.
