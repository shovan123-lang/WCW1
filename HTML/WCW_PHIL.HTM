Match observations: 

Great American Bash Tour | July 5th, 1991
Philadelphia, PA |Venue: Civic Center
Attendance: 10,000  AVG: Ticket $20
Gate: $200,000 Broadcast: Premium House Show

From the pages of the WCW Magazine:

Philadelphia has always been one of WCW's toughest towns, and the Civic Center
crowd lived up to its repuation with a loud, rowdy night of action that blended
technical excellence, wild brawls, and a main event that shook the building. 
Fans saw rising stars break through, veterans dig deep, and alliances tested
under the bright Bash tour spotlight.

Arn Anderson Survives the Dogfight!
The night opened with a battle between JYD and Arn Anderson. The match went
almost twenty minutes! JYD's power had the Enforcer reeling more than once,
but Anderson's ring IQ proved too sharp. A perfectly timed DDT ended the
fight, giving Arn a hard-earned victory and reminding everyone why he's one
of WCW's most dangerous men. 

The debut of the Bruise Brothers! 
From the pacific northwest, Ron and Don Harris make their WCW debut! In one
of the night's biggest surprises, the massive Bruise Brothers steamrolled
the Young Pistols in under six minutes. Don Harris' big boot nearly took
Steve Armstrong out of his boots, giving the newcomers a statement win. The
Pistols, fresh off big tour momentum, suddenly find themselves regrouping.

Pillman edges Liger in a Classic!
Philadelphis witnessed a near show-stealer as Brian Pillman and Jushin
Thunder Liger tore the house down in breathtaking 20 minute sprint. 
High flying, chain wrestling, and counters galore kept the fan on their
feet. Pillman snatched the win with a lightning-fast crucifix, but both meen
walked out with the crowds respect. This rivalry is far from over. 

Sullivan's dark circle strikes!
Kevin Sullivan's sinister new alliance - Mr. Hughes and One Man Gang- proved
devestating in six-man action. The Killer Bees and Dustin Rhodes fought with
heart, but Hughes' raw power sealed the deal with a crushing powerslam on Jim
Brunzell. Sullivans Dark Circle looks more dangerous every night of the tour. 

Luger Outsmarts Nikita!
Lex Luger continued his march toward dominance with a crafty win over the
Russian Nightmare. Nikita Koloff had the power advantage, but Luger used
leverage and timing to steal the victory with a rolling cradle. The win fuels
Luger's growing arrogance- and his alliance with Harley Race looms large.

Paul Orndorff Shocks Rick Steiner!
In the nights biggest upset, Mr Wonderful Paul Orndorff pinned Rick Steiner
after a brutal piledriver. The match was a hard-hitting masterpiece, with
Steiner's suplexes rattling the ring, and Orndorff answering with Veteran 
precision. The finish left fans stunned-and Orndorff hotter than ever. 

Sting and Windham topple Vader and Muta in a thriller!
The main event delivered pure WCW magic. Sting and Barry Windham battled the
monstrous Big Van Vader and the unpredictable Great Muta in a 26 minute war.
Power, speed and chaos collided until Sting soard with a stinger splash to 
pin Vader clean in the center of the ring. The civic center erupted as the
U.S. Champion stood tall, proving once again why he's the heart and soul of
WCW. Barry Windham basked in the glory of the victory, but made a gesture
that he wants to become the next WCW champion at the Great American Bash! 

The next stop for WCW is in Atlanta, Ga for WCW Saturday Night! We should
get a better idea of the card for The Great American Bash, along with
some interviews from the biggest stars.
