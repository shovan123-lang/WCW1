THE DARK CIRCLE TAPED PROMO
Atlanta, GA, Center Stage | Segment 15 (1:01:00–1:03:00)

Cut to taped footage. Total darkness. The sound of dripping water. A single
candle flickers to life, then another, then another, until a ring of candles
glows on a cold concrete floor.

Kevin Sullivan sits cross-legged in the center of the circle, his face half
hidden in shadow. Behind him, barely visible at the edge of the light, stand two
massive silhouettes: Mr. Hughes in his dark suit and sunglasses, and One Man Gang,
arms folded, motionless.

Sullivan speaks slowly, almost in a whisper.

KEVIN SULLIVAN: Do you see it? The circle. No beginning. No end. Once you step
inside... you never leave.

(He runs a finger slowly through the candle smoke.)

KEVIN SULLIVAN: In Philadelphia, the Killer Bees and Dustin Rhodes stepped inside
the circle. They fought. They struggled. They buzzed and they kicked. And in the
end... they fell. Just like everyone falls.

(Behind him, One Man Gang cracks his knuckles. Mr. Hughes doesn't move.)

KEVIN SULLIVAN: Tonight, Dustin. Tonight you come to me alone. Your father can't
save you. Your friends can't save you. You think you're the son of a legend. But
in the dark... legends mean nothing.

(He leans forward. The candlelight catches his eyes.)

KEVIN SULLIVAN: Every man in WCW should be listening. The heroes. The champions.
The giants. One by one, you will be pulled into the circle. And the circle
is hungry.

(He looks past the camera, as if seeing something far away.)

KEVIN SULLIVAN: And the circle... will grow. There are others out there. Strong.
Angry. Alone. They don't know it yet... but they already belong to us.

(Long pause.)

KEVIN SULLIVAN: Welcome... to the Dark Circle.

Sullivan leans down and blows out the candle in front of him. One by one, the rest
go dark. The last thing visible is Mr. Hughes's sunglasses reflecting the final
flame before it dies.

Black screen. Silence.

Cut back to the arena.

TONY SCHIAVONE: JR, that man gives me chills every time.

JIM ROSS: "The circle will grow." Tony, I don't know who he's talking about, but
I wouldn't want to find out. And Dustin Rhodes has to step into the ring with
Kevin Sullivan right now!
