"ROAD TO BALTIMORE": GREAT AMERICAN BASH HISTORY PACKAGE
Atlanta, GA, Center Stage | Segment 18 (1:14:30–1:20:00)

Fade in on a slow pan across the empty Baltimore Arena, seats waiting in the dark.
A single spotlight hits the center of the ring. A slow, dramatic orchestral score
plays underneath.

NARRATOR (V.O.): Every summer, the biggest stars in professional wrestling chase
one dream. The Great American Bash.

Cut to quick-cut footage of past Bash events: fireworks, packed arenas, cages being
lowered, and champions raising gold.

NARRATOR (V.O.): It's where rivalries are settled. It's where history is written.
Right here in Atlanta, at the Omni in 1987, the Bash gave birth to the most
dangerous match ever created: WarGames.

Cut to footage of the double-ring WarGames cage, bodies flying, the crowd going
wild.

NARRATOR (V.O.): But one city has become the home of champions. Baltimore,
Maryland.

Cut to footage of the Baltimore Arena, 1988. A younger Lex Luger flexes in the ring
across from Ric Flair.

NARRATOR (V.O.): 1988. The Baltimore Arena. Lex Luger stood across the ring from
the World Heavyweight Champion with the title within his reach. He had Flair beaten.
He had the crowd on its feet. But the night ended without the gold, on a decision
Lex Luger has never forgotten.

Cut to a slow-motion shot of Luger on his knees, bleeding, staring at the title
he didn't win.

NARRATOR (V.O.): Three years of frustration. Three years of coming close.

Cut to footage of the Baltimore Arena, 1990. Sting climbs the turnbuckle, then
footage of Sting holding the title high as confetti falls.

NARRATOR (V.O.): 1990. Same city. Same building. Sting turned the Bash into the
greatest night of his life and walked out of Baltimore as World Heavyweight
Champion.

Cut to black. A beat of silence. Then a cage drops into frame with a loud metallic
crash.

NARRATOR (V.O.): Now, in 1991, the throne is empty. There's no Ric Flair. There's
no champion. There's only a steel cage... and two men who believe they were born
for this moment.

Cut to Lex Luger posing in front of a mirror, then turning toward the camera with a
cold stare. Harley Race stands behind him.

LEX LUGER: (pre-taped) Baltimore owes me. Three years ago they took that title from
me. On July 14th, I take it back. And this time nobody, NOBODY, is stopping me.

Cut to Barry Windham in a dark locker room, taping his wrists.

BARRY WINDHAM: (pre-taped) I've stood in the shadows of a lot of great men. I've
watched other people hold that belt. Not anymore. When that cage door shuts,
Luger, there's nowhere left to hide. Baltimore is where champions are made, and
I'm gonna be the next one.

Cut to the cage lowering over the ring, then a slow push in on the new World
Heavyweight Championship belt.

NARRATOR (V.O.): Lex Luger. Barry Windham. One steel cage. One vacant World
Heavyweight Championship. The Great American Bash. Sunday, July 14th. Baltimore.
Where champions are made.

Hard cut to black.
