Cold open. No pyro and no theme music. The camera fades up on an empty ring under 
the house lights. 

In the center stands a small table draped in black cloth with something covered 
beneath it. The crowd murmurs. Somewhere in the back rows a chant starts and grows:
“WE WANT FLAIR! WE WANT FLAIR!”

JIM ROSS (V.O.): Ladies and gentlemen, welcome to 
WCW Saturday Night. Normally we’d open this program with a bang, but tonight is 
different. Tonight, there’s business to attend to.



TONY SCHIAVONE (V.O.): JR, I’ve been doing this a long time and I’ve never seen a 
building feel like this. Listen to these people. 

Executive Vice President Jim Herd
walks briskly down the aisle in a dark suit, holding a folded sheet of paper. 
The chants turn to loud boos as he climbs the steps. He stands behind the covered
table and waits for silence that never comes. 



JIM HERD: Ladies and gentlemen… (boos) …ladies and gentlemen, I’m here tonight to 
make an official statement on behalf of World Championship Wrestling.


(A sign in the front row reads “NO FLAIR, NO FANS.”)



HERD: As many of you have heard, Ric Flair is no longer under contract with WCW.
We negotiated in good faith. We could not reach an agreement. Mr. Flair has chosen
to leave this company.

(The “WE WANT FLAIR!” chant explodes. Herd raises a hand 
and is ignored.)



HERD: And I’ll be honest with you. When Mr. Flair left, he took with him property
that belongs to this company. (The crowd roars its approval, and Herd’s jaw 
tightens.) That belt he’s carrying around is a piece of leather and gold. 
It is not the World Heavyweight Championship. It hasn’t been since the day he 
walked out that door.



JR (V.O.): Tony, did I just hear that right? Flair left with the title belt?


TONY (V.O.): That’s what the man said, 
JR. Ric Flair took the Big Gold Belt with him!



HERD: Because the championship isn’t a belt. The championship is World Championship
Wrestling. And no one man… (boos) …no one man is bigger than WCW.


Herd grabs the black cloth and pulls it away. Underneath sits a brand-new
World Heavyweight Championship belt, freshly made, with a large gold center plate
and “WCW” across the top. 

Some fans clap politely, more of them boo, and one loud voice yells 
“THAT’S NOT THE REAL ONE!”

HERD: This is the new symbol of World Championship 
Wrestling. 

Effective immediately, the WCW World Heavyweight Championship is vacant. 
On Sunday, July 14th, at the Great American Bash in Baltimore, Lex Luger and 
Barry Windham will meet inside a steel cage, and the winner will be the first man 
to wear this championship.

(The reaction is mixed. There are cheers for the match,
with Flair chants still breaking through.)



HERD: There will be no disqualifications, no count-outs and no excuses. 
Two men go in, one man comes out the champion. The future of WCW starts in 
Baltimore.

Herd turns and leaves without taking questions, and the boos follow him 
to the curtain. The camera pushes in slowly on the new belt, alone on the table 
under the lights.



JR (V.O.): Well, there it is. A new belt, a vacant title, and a lot of unanswered 
questions.



TONY (V.O.): These fans aren’t buying it yet, JR. But whoever walks out of that cage
in Baltimore has a chance to make that belt mean something.



JR (V.O.): And one of those men, Barry Windham, will be here later tonight. 
But right now, let’s get to the ring! 

Jushin “Thunder” Liger is in action! 

Theme music hits. Cut to Liger’s entrance.
