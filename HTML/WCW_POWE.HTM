Match observations: 

Nutter Center
Dayton, OH
July 17, 1991
Attendance: 7,000
Average ticket: $9.00
Gate: $63,000
Merchandise: $10,500 

WINDHAM DISQUALIFIED AS THE CHAMPION STRIKES

Lex Luger's first week as World Heavyweight Champion brought him to the
Nutter Center in Dayton, and he didn't come alone. With Harley Race and Mr.
Hughes beside him, the new champion told 7,000 fans that the old Lex Luger
is gone. By the end of the night Barry Windham had found out what that
means.

NOBODY GETS IN

Luger held the new belt up to the camera and said he won it alone, inside a
steel cage in Baltimore, and that a champion protects what's his. Race
delivered the warning: nobody gets in and nobody gets out, not without
going through them. Race had already made himself felt earlier in the
night. He got involved in Big Van Vader's match with Duane Gill, and Vader
flattened Gill with the Vader Bomb in 3:06.

WINDHAM SEES RED

The main event was Windham's chance to get his hands on the champion's
camp, and for ten minutes he had Mr. Hughes in trouble. Then Luger came off
the floor and jumped him. Windham went after the champion, the referee lost
control of the match, and at 10:07 Windham was disqualified. Mr. Hughes
gets the win.

It got stranger from there. One Man Gang stormed the ring and went after
Hughes. Fans will remember that Hughes walked out on the Dark Circle on WCW
Pro just days ago. Was the Gang settling that score? The Dark Circle isn't
saying.

SIMMONS FLATTENS ARN

Ron Simmons scored the biggest win of his summer when he drove Arn Anderson
into the mat with the spinebuster at 9:50. Simmons has made it plain that
he wants Luger, and wins like this put him in line.

Arn was still on the mat when Paul E. Dangerously came down the aisle,
helped him to his feet and pressed a business card into his hand. "Alone,
you lose," Paul E. told him. Arn let the card fall to the mat and walked
out. Paul E. didn't look worried.

PAUL E. STRIKES AGAIN

Brian Pillman and Bobby Eaton were building toward a big finish when Paul
E. got involved one more time. The referee had seen enough and disqualified
Eaton at 8:25. Pillman wins, but Paul E. keeps finding ways to make himself
the story.

AROUND THE RING

The Young Pistols beat Dick Murdoch and Butch Reed when Tracy Smothers
rolled up Reed at 7:07. The Bruise Brothers needed only 4:38 to dispose of
Duke Myers and Eli the Eliminator, and Don Harris finished Eli with the big
boot. El Gigante chokeslammed Ozz for the win at 4:06.
