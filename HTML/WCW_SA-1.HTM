Match observations: 

RUDE AWAKENING IN GREENVILLE
WCW Saturday Night | Greenville, SC | July 13, 1991

WCW Saturday Night rolled into the Memorial Auditorium in Greenville, South
Carolina, one night before the Great American Bash. It will be remembered
for one ugly scene.

Dustin Rhodes came out to tell the fans about his neck. The son of the
American Dream has been on the shelf since Columbus, and he wanted everyone
to know he is coming back. He never finished the sentence. Paul E.
Dangerously walked onto the set with Rick Rude and Bobby Eaton behind him.
Eaton took Dustin from behind, and Rude dropped him with the Rude
Awakening, right on the injured neck.

Rick Steiner hit the set at a dead run and went after anything in a suit or
tights. Paul E. and his men backed up the aisle, but the damage was done.
Rude and Steiner meet tomorrow night in Baltimore, and Steiner has more
than one score to settle now.

In the main event, Big Van Vader and the Great Muta beat the Killer Bees
when Vader pinned Jim Brunzell after the Vader Attack at 10:38. The Bees
had the big man reeling until Harley Race got involved against B. Brian
Blair. Earlier in the night Race was introduced as Lex Luger's official
manager, and with Mr. Hughes beside him he promised that Luger walks out of
the cage as World Champion.

Bobby Eaton started his evening by pinning Bill Irwin with the Alabama Jam
in 5:51, and Terry Taylor needed only 4:30 to put Black Bart away with the
Five Arm.

The Fabulous Freebirds and Diamond Dallas Page ran down the Young Pistols
and Big Josh, who answered right back. Paul Orndorff told the Junk Yard Dog
he has one night left as a main-eventer. The Brian Pillman and Jushin Liger
rematch got a full video package.

Sting and Nikita Koloff came face to face one last time before their U.S.
Title match, and neither man gave an inch. The show closed with final words
from Barry Windham and Lex Luger. One of them leaves Baltimore as WCW World
Heavyweight Champion. The other may not leave under his own power.
