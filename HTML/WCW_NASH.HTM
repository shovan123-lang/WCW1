KOLOFF KEEPS IT, LUGER COUNTED OUT

Harley Race says Lex Luger gets his rematch every town, every night.
Nashville was the first town, and Luger didn't make it back into the
ring.

Nikita Koloff and Luger fought all over the Music City Center for 16
minutes, with Race and Mr. Hughes barking at the referee from ringside.
Luger had Koloff hurt with a clothesline and went for the press, but
Koloff fought out of it and backdropped him over the top rope. Luger hit
the floor hard, and Hughes was arguing with Race over what to do next
when the referee reached ten. Koloff wins by count-out at 16:21 and is
still the World Heavyweight Champion. Luger threw a chair into the ring
on his way to the back. Inglewood is tomorrow night.

PAUL E. IS EVERYWHERE

Paul E. Dangerously didn't wrestle in Nashville, but he decided three
matches.

He went after Brian Pillman on the floor, and Larry Zbyszko kept
Pillman out there long enough to be counted out at 14:05. Zbyszko and
Paul E. left together, smiling.

He climbed the apron when Tommy Rich had Arn Anderson set up, and Arn
caught Rich with the double-arm DDT and pinned him at 14:09. Arn looked
at Paul E. for a long moment before he left.

And in the tag title match, Paul E. got in Tracy Smothers' way, the
Freebirds took liberties behind the referee, and Michael Hayes was
disqualified at 18:51 for refusing to break. The Young Pistols won, but
the Fabulous Freebirds keep the World Tag Team Titles.

THE REST OF THE CARD

Rick Steiner beat the Great Muta clean in 8:07, pinning him after a
running bulldog.

Robert Gibson rolled up Diamond Dallas Page at 13:03. Ron Simmons got
involved against Page and Kimberly got involved against Gibson, but
Gibson got the three.

El Gigante and One Man Gang fought to a double count-out at 9:46 and
kept swinging at each other all the way to the locker room.

The Junk Yard Dog beat Dick Murdoch with the Thump powerslam at 7:21.

The Killer Bees opened the show by beating Bill Irwin and Butch Reed in
10:10 when Jim Brunzell pinned Irwin with a dropkick.
