“MR. WONDERFUL” PAUL ORNDORFF PROFILE
Atlanta, GA, Center Stage | Segment 9 (33:30–35:30)

Fade in on slow-motion footage of Paul Orndorff walking to the ring in Philadelphia, 
robe on and sunglasses down, sneering at the crowd. A swaggering guitar riff plays 
underneath.

NARRATOR (V.O.): Some men are made champions. Some men are made legends. 
And then there’s the man who has always believed he was born better than everyone 
else.

Cut to old black-and-white photos of a young, muscular Orndorff in a football 
uniform.

NARRATOR (V.O.): From Brandon, Florida, Paul Orndorff was a punishing running 
back at the University of Tampa, so tough that the New Orleans Saints drafted him 
to play on Sundays. But football couldn’t hold him. He wanted the spotlight all to 
himself.

Cut to grainy footage of Orndorff flexing in the ring, then hitting a piledriver 
on an opponent.

NARRATOR (V.O.): In wrestling he found his calling, and his weapon: the piledriver. 
One of the most feared moves in the sport.

Cut to footage from the mid-1980s showing a packed arena, Orndorff in the 
main event, and huge crowds on their feet.

NARRATOR (V.O.): He headlined the biggest events in the world and went toe-to-toe 
with the most famous names this sport has ever known. Wherever he went, 
Paul Orndorff was the man people paid to see, whether they loved him or hated him.

Cut to a close-up interview shot. Orndorff sits in a leather chair in a dark studio
with a single light on him and a slight smirk.

PAUL ORNDORFF: People ask me why they call me “Mr. Wonderful.” (He leans forward.) 
Look at me. Do I really have to explain it?

Cut to Philadelphia footage from two nights ago: Orndorff and Rick Steiner trading 
blows, Steiner hitting a belly-to-belly suplex, and the crowd going crazy.

NARRATOR (V.O.): Then, two nights ago in Philadelphia, he announced his arrival 
in WCW the only way he knows how.

Slow-motion replay: Orndorff catches Steiner, hoists him up and plants him with 
the piledriver. The referee counts three.

NARRATOR (V.O.): He took everything Rick Steiner had, and then he took Steiner 
out.

Back to the studio interview.

PAUL ORNDORFF: Rick Steiner’s a tough man, I’ll give him that. But tough doesn’t 
beat wonderful. (He taps his own chest.) Nobody in this locker room is on my level. 
Not Steiner, not the Junk Yard Dog, nobody. You want to know who the best wrestler 
in WCW is? (He puts on his sunglasses.) You’re looking at him.

Freeze-frame on Orndorff’s smirk. Gold text slams across the screen:

“MR. WONDERFUL” PAUL ORNDORFF
WCW

Cut to black, then back to the interview area for segment 10.

TONY SCHIAVONE (live): Well, JR, he certainly doesn’t lack confidence.

JIM ROSS: Confidence or arrogance, Tony, he backed it up in Philadelphia. 
And speaking of Mr. Wonderful, he’s standing by with Eric Bischoff right now…

Cut to segment 10: the Orndorff interview, with JYD interrupting.
