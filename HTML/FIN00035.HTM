El Gigante makes the SAVE
Atlanta, GA, Center Stage | Segment 17 (1:10:00–1:11:30)

The referee calls for the bell as Mr. Hughes and One Man Gang storm the ring and
attack Dustin Rhodes. The match is thrown out, but the Dark Circle doesn't care.
Hughes holds Dustin's arms while One Man Gang drives a massive forearm into his
chest. Kevin Sullivan stands in the corner, grinning and pointing at Dustin's head.

TONY SCHIAVONE: Dustin Rhodes wins by disqualification, but look at this, JR!
Three-on-one!

JIM ROSS: This is a mugging, Tony! Somebody get out here!

Sullivan pulls something from his boot and raises it high. The crowd boos as One
Man Gang lifts Dustin up for the 747 splash in the corner...

The lights flicker. A low, booming horn sounds. The crowd turns toward the
entrance and ROARS as a giant figure walks through the curtain.

TONY SCHIAVONE: It's El Gigante! El Gigante is here!

JIM ROSS: Seven foot seven, Tony, and he's coming fast!

El Gigante steps over the top rope without breaking stride. Mr. Hughes swings at
him and Gigante catches the punch, then swats Hughes across the ring with one open
hand. Hughes tumbles out to the floor.

One Man Gang charges. The two giants collide in the middle of the ring and the
whole building shakes. Gigante grabs OMG by the throat, walks him backward to the
ropes and HOISTS him up and over the top rope. Four hundred pounds crash to the
arena floor.

JIM ROSS: OVER THE TOP! El Gigante just threw One Man Gang out of the ring like he
was nothing!

Sullivan backs into the corner, then rolls under the bottom rope and scrambles to
his partners on the floor. Gigante stands tall in the center of the ring, then
reaches down and helps Dustin Rhodes to his feet. The Center Stage crowd is on
its feet.

One Man Gang pulls himself up by the ring apron, glaring at Gigante. He points up
at the giant and slams his fist into his palm. Gigante steps to the ropes, points
straight back at him, then holds up one finger: one more time.

TONY SCHIAVONE: JR, those two are gonna collide again, and I think we know where.

JIM ROSS: El Gigante and One Man Gang at the Great American Bash, Tony. And Kevin
Sullivan just found out the Dark Circle has a problem it can't scare away.

Cut to break.
