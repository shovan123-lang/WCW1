TOO LATE: RICK STEINER MAKES THE SAVE
Greenville, Memorial Auditorium | Segment 8 (2:00)

Rick Steiner comes charging down the aisle in his headgear and a WCW
T-shirt. Rude sees him coming and rolls out the far side of the ring.
Paul E. and Eaton scramble after him, and the three of them backpedal up
the aisle as Steiner slides in.

JIM ROSS: Here comes Rick Steiner! But he's too late, Tony! The damage is
done!

Steiner kneels over Dustin and waves frantically for help. Paramedics
hurry down with a backboard and a stretcher. They strap Dustin in and
roll him up the aisle while the crowd applauds. Steiner grabs a
microphone and turns toward the stage, where Rude is still posing.

RICK STEINER: Rude! That's your big statement? You jumped a kid in a neck
brace, three on one, with your manager holding a telephone? Tomorrow
night there's no brace. There's no three on one. It's you and me. And
I'm bringing everything I've got.

RICK STEINER: Ask Arn Anderson what I did to him this morning. Ask him
how that Steiner Bulldog felt. Tomorrow night in Baltimore, you're
gonna find out, pretty boy. And I don't care how many phones Paul E.
brings with him.

Steiner throws down the microphone. Rude smirks and points at him from the
stage, then wipes his brow with Dustin's neck brace, which Eaton has
grabbed back from a fan.

JIM ROSS: Rick Steiner and Rick Rude, tomorrow night. And after what we
just saw, I don't know if any referee can hold Steiner back.
