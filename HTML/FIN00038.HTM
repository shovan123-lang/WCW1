NIKITA KOLOFF & STING FACE TO FACE
Atlanta, GA, Center Stage | Segment 20 (1:27:00–1:32:00)

Cut to the ring. Tony Schiavone stands in the center of the ring with a microphone.

TONY SCHIAVONE: Ladies and gentlemen, at the Great American Bash, Sting will
defend the United States Championship against the Russian Nightmare, Nikita
Koloff. Right now, I'd like to bring out the challenger. Nikita Koloff!

Koloff's music hits. He walks to the ring slowly, a heavy chain draped around his
neck. He doesn't acknowledge the crowd. He takes the microphone from Schiavone
without a word.

NIKITA KOLOFF: Two nights ago in Philadelphia, Lex Luger beat me. He rolled me up
like a schoolboy. He didn't beat me with power. He didn't beat me with strength.
He beat me with a trick.

(He wraps the chain tighter around his fist.)

NIKITA KOLOFF: And all week, what do I hear? Nobody talks about Nikita. They talk
about Luger. They talk about Windham. And always, always they talk about Sting.
The face of WCW. The people's champion.

NIKITA KOLOFF: I am done being overlooked. In Baltimore, I will take every ounce
of anger from Philadelphia and I will put it on Sting. And I will take the United
States Championship home with me.

Sting's music hits. The crowd ERUPTS as Sting sprints down the aisle with the U.S.
Championship over his shoulder. He slides into the ring and stops a few feet from
Koloff. He takes a microphone.

STING: Nikita... I hear you, man. And you know what? You're right about one thing.
Luger got lucky in Philly. But don't you dare bring that frustration into MY ring
and think I'm gonna lie down for it.

NIKITA KOLOFF: I don't want you to lie down. I want you to fight.

STING: (stepping closer) Oh, you're gonna get a fight. You want the United States
Championship? (He holds up the belt.) Then you're gonna have to go through the
Stinger to get it. And I've never backed down from anybody, not Vader, not Muta,
and not you.

The two stand nose to nose. The crowd is on its feet. Koloff slowly raises the
chain. Sting doesn't flinch. For a long moment, nobody moves.

Koloff lowers the chain and steps back, a cold smile on his face.

NIKITA KOLOFF: Save your energy, Sting. You are going to need it in Baltimore.

He drops the microphone and walks out of the ring. Sting watches him leave, then
raises the U.S. Title high as the crowd chants his name.

STING: (into the microphone) Baltimore, it's SHOWTIME! WHOOOOOO!

Cut to ringside.

JIM ROSS: Tony, you could cut that tension with a knife. Nikita Koloff is a man
with a grudge, and Sting is a man who refuses to back down.

TONY SCHIAVONE: And it all comes to a head at the Great American Bash, JR. Sting
and Koloff for the United States Championship!

Cut to break.
