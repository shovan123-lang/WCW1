THE ROAD TO THE CAGE
Baltimore, Baltimore Arena | Segment 15 (2:00)

The arena lights go down and a video package plays on the big screen.
Footage of Jim Herd at the podium in Atlanta, holding up the new World
Heavyweight Championship belt.

JIM ROSS (voiceover): Atlanta, July 6th. With Ric Flair gone, Jim Herd
unveiled a new World Heavyweight Championship, and the hunt began.

Footage of Barry Windham superplexing Ozz, then grabbing the microphone.

JIM ROSS (voiceover): That same night, Barry Windham made his choice. He
called out Lex Luger, and he asked for a cage, so Harley Race and Mr.
Hughes couldn't save him.

Footage from Columbia: Luger and Hughes beating Sting and Windham by DQ.
Then Charleston: Windham laying out Hughes with a lariat while Luger
watches from ringside.

JIM ROSS (voiceover): They've traded shots all over the Carolinas. Now
there's nowhere left to go.

The package ends. The arena stays dark except for a spotlight on the
cage as it slowly comes down from the rafters. The crowd counts it down
to the mat. The ring crew locks the panels and chains the door.

TONY SCHIAVONE: There it is, Jim. Fifteen feet of steel, one door, and
one belt.

JIM ROSS: Two men go in. One man comes out the World Heavyweight
Champion.
