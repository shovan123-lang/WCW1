MICHAEL HAYES INJURY UPDATE / JIMMY GARVIN PROMO
Atlanta, GA, Center Stage | Segment 13 (53:30–56:00)

Cut to the broadcast desk. Tony Schiavone holds a sheet of paper.

TONY SCHIAVONE: Welcome back to WCW Saturday Night. Folks, we've got an update on
"P.S." Michael Hayes, who has been on the shelf with an injury and away from his
Freebird partner Jimmy Garvin. JR, I'm happy to report the news is good.

JIM ROSS: It sure is, Tony. WCW doctors examined Michael Hayes earlier this week,
and he has been medically cleared to return to the ring. Hayes will make his
comeback this Monday night at the Columbus Civic Center in Columbus, Georgia.

TONY SCHIAVONE: Jimmy Garvin has been holding down the fort by himself, and he
joins us now.

Cut to the interview area. Jimmy Garvin stands in his Freebird gear, sunglasses
pushed up on his head, a Confederate-gray Freebirds jacket over his shoulder. He
grins at the camera.

JIMMY GARVIN: Well, well, well. Did y'all miss us? 'Cause I'll tell you right now,
WCW sure has been quiet without the Fabulous Freebirds.

(He holds up two fingers.)

JIMMY GARVIN: For weeks, people have been comin' up to me sayin', "Jimmy, where's
Michael? Jimmy, are the Freebirds done?" Done? Brother, the Freebirds don't get
DONE. We get knocked down, we get back up, and we get even.

JIMMY GARVIN: Michael's been sittin' at home watchin' every single show, and he's
been takin' notes. Dustin Rhodes. The Young Pistols. Y'all been runnin' around
this company like you own the place while the Birds were grounded. Well, I've
got news for you.

(He leans into the camera.)

JIMMY GARVIN: Monday night in Columbus, Michael Hayes is back. And come July 14th
at the Great American Bash in Baltimore, we're gonna show Dustin and those two
little Pistols exactly what happens when the Freebirds are whole again.

JIMMY GARVIN: Columbus, warm up those vocal cords, darlin'. Badstreet is open for
business. The Freebirds fly again!

He drops his sunglasses down over his eyes, gives a flick of the hair and walks
off camera.

Cut back to the desk.

TONY SCHIAVONE: The Freebirds reunite Monday in Columbus, and JR, they've got a
big six-man at the Bash.

JIM ROSS: They do, Tony. And still to come tonight, a message from "Ravishing" Rick
Rude. Stay with us.
