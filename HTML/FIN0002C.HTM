BRUISE BROTHERS POST-MATCH: THE SAVE

Atlanta, GA, Center Stage | Segment 8 (29:00–30:30)



The bell rings and it’s over. Don Harris has just flattened Bill Irwin with the big 
boot, and Ron pins him easily. 

Their music hits, but the Bruise Brothers aren’t finished.

 Butch Reed is dazed and
trying to pull himself up in the corner. Ron grabs him by the hair and drags him 
back to the middle of the ring.



TONY SCHIAVONE: The match is over! Somebody tell these two the match is over!



JIM ROSS: They know, Tony. They just don’t care.

Don lifts Irwin into a vicious 
backbreaker and drops him across his knee. Ron goes out to ringside, pulls a steel 
chair from under the apron and slides it into the ring. Officials pour into the 
ring, but Ron shoves the first referee hard into the turnbuckle.



JIM ROSS: Oh, come on! Get some help out here! They’re gonna hurt this man!


Don sets the chair down in the middle of the ring. Ron hoists Irwin up for a 
piledriver onto the steel. The crowd boos loudly, and then…

Music hits. 
The crowd ERUPTS as PN News comes charging down the aisle, all 400-plus pounds of 
him, with Tommy “Wildfire” Rich right behind him!



TONY SCHIAVONE: It’s PN News! And Tommy Rich is with him!



JIM ROSS: Here comes the cavalry! 

Ron drops Irwin and turns just in time to eat a 
running splash from News that knocks him flat. Rich slides in and unloads a flurry 
of Georgia-style right hands on Don, driving him back toward the ropes. Don 
lunges forward and catches a Thesz press from Rich!

The Bruise Brothers regroup on 
the floor. Ron grabs the chair, but News snatches it out of his hands and holds it
up, daring them to come back in. The Atlanta crowd is on its feet.



JIM ROSS: Big man’s got the chair, and the Bruise Brothers want no part of it!

The 
Harris brothers back slowly up the aisle, staring daggers at News and Rich. 
Don points at them and mouths, “You’re next.” Ron runs a finger across his throat.


In the ring, Rich kneels down to check on Bill Irwin while News stands guard, 
still holding the chair. He grabs a microphone.



PN NEWS: (breathing hard) Yo, Bruise Brothers! Y’all wanna pick on somebody? 
Pick on us! Baltimore! The Great American Bash! Me and Wildfire, we’ll be waitin’!


The crowd roars. Rich stands up and points defiantly up the aisle as the Bruise 
Brothers disappear through the curtain.



TONY SCHIAVONE: JR, did you hear that? PN News just challenged the Bruise Brothers 
for the Great American Bash!



JIM ROSS: He sure did, Tony, and I’ll tell you what, that’s gonna be a collision. 
You’ve got two of the biggest, meanest men we’ve seen in a long time against a 
400-pound rap master and one of the toughest men ever to come out of Georgia. 
Baltimore just got a whole lot more interesting!



Cut to break.
