PAUL E. DANGEROUSLY: "I MADE AN INVESTMENT"
Tallahassee, Leon County Civic Center | Segment 5 (2:00)

Eric Bischoff stands in front of the WCW backdrop. Paul E. Dangerously
walks in talking on his portable phone, holds up a finger to Bischoff,
and finishes his call.

PAUL E. DANGEROUSLY: No, no. You tell him the price went up. Click.

He snaps the antenna down.

ERIC BISCHOFF: Paul E., all week you've been at ringside for Bobby
Eaton, and in Richmond you were at ringside for Rick Rude when he beat
Dustin Rhodes. People want to know what you're up to.

PAUL E. DANGEROUSLY: Eric, I'm going to explain something to you slowly,
because I know you're a television announcer. Wrestlers come and go.
Champions come and go. Ric Flair came and went. But a man who knows
what talent is worth? That man stays. I don't manage wrestlers. I make
investments.

ERIC BISCHOFF: Investments.

PAUL E. DANGEROUSLY: Bobby Eaton was the most underrated man in this
company for ten years, until I picked up the phone. Now he's winning.
Ravishing Rick Rude was flying back and forth to Japan with nobody in
this country paying attention, until I picked up the phone. Now he
beats Dustin Rhodes, and on Sunday he takes Rick Steiner apart at the
Great American Bash.

He leans into the camera.

PAUL E. DANGEROUSLY: And to every so-called hero in that locker room who
thinks I'm just a loudmouth with a telephone: Saturday night, in
Greenville, South Carolina, you are going to see exactly what an
investment looks like. And you are going to see what it costs the people
who get in the way.

He pulls the antenna back up, starts dialing and walks off without
another word. Bischoff looks at the camera.

ERIC BISCHOFF: Saturday night, Greenville. Back to you, Tony.
