SATURDAY NIGHT RECAP
Atlanta, CNN Center studio | Segment 2 (5:00)

JR and Tony at the desk. Footage from WCW Saturday Night, Greenville,
South Carolina, 7/13.

JIM ROSS: Before we go on, Tony, we have to show the fans what happened
last Saturday night in Greenville. If you missed it, you need to see it.
If you saw it, you need to see it again.

Footage: Dustin Rhodes on the ramp, talking about the Bash. Paul E.,
Rick Rude and Bobby Eaton come from behind.

TONY SCHIAVONE: Dustin Rhodes had already been hurt. He wrestled
Jushin Liger to a twenty-minute draw in Columbus with a bad neck, and
he came out in Greenville to tell the fans he'd be back. He never got
the chance to finish.

Footage: Rude and Eaton stomp Dustin. Rude lifts him and drops him with
the Rude Awakening, neck first.

JIM ROSS: Rick Rude dropped him with the Rude Awakening, right on that
injured neck. And Paul E. Dangerously stood over him and smiled.

Footage: Rick Steiner sprints down the ramp, clears Rude and Eaton out
of the ring, and kneels next to Dustin.

TONY SCHIAVONE: Rick Steiner got there in time. Thank goodness for
that. Dustin Rhodes is out of action, and nobody can tell us when he'll
be back.

JIM ROSS: And then came Baltimore.

Footage: the Great American Bash, 7/14. The cage lowers. Luger's
piledriver on Windham. Jim Herd hands Luger the belt.

TONY SCHIAVONE: Lex Luger beat Barry Windham inside the cage for the
vacant World Heavyweight Championship.

JIM ROSS: And he held it four days. Thursday night in Cleveland, Nikita
Koloff cinched in the front facelock, and Lex Luger went out cold.
Nikita Koloff is the World Heavyweight Champion.

Footage: Koloff with the belt over his head, Harley Race shouting at
the referee.

TONY SCHIAVONE: And last night in Charleston, West Virginia, in Sting's
last scheduled match in WCW, Rick Rude beat him for the United States
Championship, with Paul E. in the middle of it again.

Footage: Rude's facebuster on Sting. Paul E. raises Rude's arm.

JIM ROSS: Paul E. Dangerously has a United States Champion now, Tony,
and he's not done shopping. Bobby Eaton is in our main event today. If
I'm Tommy Rich, I'm keeping one eye on that telephone.
