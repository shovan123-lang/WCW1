PAUL ORNDORFF INTERVIEW / JUNK YARD DOG INTERRUPTS
Atlanta, GA, Center Stage | Segment 10 (35:30–39:30)

Cut to the interview area. Eric Bischoff stands in front of the WCW backdrop,
microphone in hand. Paul Orndorff strolls into frame in a sequined robe, sunglasses
on, chewing gum and taking his time.

ERIC BISCHOFF: Ladies and gentlemen, I'm here with a man who made one heck of a
statement in Philadelphia two nights ago. Paul Orndorff, you pinned Rick Steiner in
your first night in WCW. A lot of people are calling it the upset of the year.

PAUL ORNDORFF: (lowering his sunglasses) Upset? Bischoff, an upset is when somebody
who isn't supposed to win, wins. I was always supposed to win. The only people who
were upset were the ones who bet against Mr. Wonderful.

ERIC BISCHOFF: Rick Steiner threw everything he had at you. Those suplexes rattled
the whole building.

PAUL ORNDORFF: And I'm still standing here looking like this. (He gestures to
himself.) Steiner's somewhere icing his neck. You see, Eric, that's the difference.
Everybody in that locker room is wrestling for a paycheck. I'm wrestling for history.
And right now, history says there isn't a man in WCW who can stand up to the
piledriver.

ERIC BISCHOFF: So who's next for Paul Orndorff?

PAUL ORNDORFF: Who's next? (He laughs.) That's the problem, Bischoff. I looked up
and down this roster, and I don't see anybody worth my time. Nobody...

A familiar bark echoes off camera. The crowd recognizes it instantly and roars.
The Junk Yard Dog walks into frame, chain around his neck, and stops nose to nose
with Orndorff. Bischoff takes a step back.

JUNK YARD DOG: Nobody, huh? Well, look again, Mr. Wonderful, 'cause the Dog is
right here.

PAUL ORNDORFF: (smirking) Well, well. Look who it is. Hey, Dog, weren't you the one
Arn Anderson planted face-first with a DDT in Philadelphia? I watched it from the
back. Real touching. You fought hard, and then you lost.

JUNK YARD DOG: That's right. I lost. I went twenty minutes with the Enforcer and I
came up short, and I ain't makin' no excuses about it. But I'll tell you what I
ain't gonna do. I ain't gonna stand around and listen to some pretty boy in a
bathrobe tell the world there's nobody in WCW who can beat him.

The crowd cheers. Orndorff's smile tightens.

JUNK YARD DOG: You talkin' about history? I been bitin' and scratchin' in this
business since you were still tryin' to catch footballs. You wanna know who's next?
The Dog is next. Me and you. The Great American Bash. And when I'm done, they're
gonna be callin' you "Mr. Wonder-what-happened."

The crowd explodes. Orndorff pulls off his sunglasses and slowly folds them, never
taking his eyes off JYD.

PAUL ORNDORFF: You know something, Dog? You've got a lot of heart. I respect that.
(beat) But heart doesn't stop the piledriver. You want me in Baltimore? You got me.
(He steps in close.) Baltimore... if you can still walk.

Orndorff shoves the sunglasses into JYD's chest and walks off. JYD lets them fall
to the floor, then crushes them under his boot. He turns to the camera and barks
loudly as the crowd chants "DOG! DOG! DOG!"

ERIC BISCHOFF: (to camera) You heard it, folks! Paul Orndorff against the Junk Yard
Dog, at the Great American Bash in Baltimore! JR, Tony, back to you!

Cut to ringside.

JIM ROSS: Well, Tony, the Junk Yard Dog may have lost to Arn Anderson, but he's
not backing down from anybody.

TONY SCHIAVONE: And Orndorff just accepted. JR, Mr. Wonderful against the Dog in
Baltimore. That's a fight!

JIM ROSS: Up next, the Rock 'n' Roll Express are in action. Stay with us!
