WCW MAGAZINE: WCW PRO REPORT
Tallahassee, Florida | Leon County Civic Center | Aired July 13, 1991

THE DOG-FACED GREMLIN DRAWS FIRST BLOOD

The WCW Television Championship is up for grabs, and Rick Steiner made
sure everyone knows he wants it. In the main event of WCW Pro, Steiner
went twelve rugged minutes with the Enforcer, Arn Anderson, in the first
round of the TV Title tournament. Arn went after Steiner's knee from the
opening bell and seemed to have him grounded, but Steiner kept getting
up. When he finally caught Arn coming off the ropes, he hit the Steiner
Bulldog and pinned him at 12:26.

Steiner moves on to the semifinals. He has bigger business tomorrow
night, though: Ravishing Rick Rude at the Great American Bash.

MR. WONDERFUL ROLLS ON

Paul Orndorff wasted no time in the other quarterfinal. Terry Taylor
gave him a fight, but Orndorff dropped him with the piledriver at 8:32,
and Mr. Wonderful strutted out of the ring like he already owns the
belt. Next for Orndorff is the winner of Larry Zbyszko vs. Robert Gibson.
Steiner waits for the winner of Tommy Rich vs. Bobby Eaton.

PAUL E. HAS A WARNING

Paul E. Dangerously told Eric Bischoff he doesn't manage wrestlers. He
makes investments. He took credit for Bobby Eaton's summer and for Rick
Rude's win over Dustin Rhodes in Richmond, and then he promised that
Saturday night in Greenville the whole locker room will see "what an
investment looks like."

Dustin Rhodes heard him. In a taped interview, wearing a neck brace,
Dustin admitted he won't be cleared for Baltimore and won't say how long
he'll be out. But he said he'll be in Greenville tonight to face the
fans himself, and he told Paul E. to say it to his face.

THE CIRCLE IS BROKEN

Kevin Sullivan has vanished. Mr. Hughes and the One Man Gang came to the
ring in Tallahassee, lit the candles and waited for their leader. He
never came. Then Mr. Hughes shook his head and walked out, leaving OMG
alone in the middle of the candles. Has Hughes chosen Lex Luger? And who
will lead what's left of the Dark Circle?

AROUND THE RING

The Killer Bees beat Dick Murdoch and Butch Reed when B. Brian Blair
rolled up Murdoch with a bodyscissors cradle at 7:52. Murdoch insisted
the man who pinned him wasn't the man who started the match, and he may
have a point. Barry Windham tuned up for the cage with a superplex on Dana
McKinney, and the Fabulous Freebirds took care of the Twilight Zone with
Michael Hayes getting the pin.

THE GREAT AMERICAN BASH: SUNDAY, JULY 14, BALTIMORE ARENA

Steel Cage, for the vacant WCW World Title: Lex Luger vs. Barry Windham.
U.S. Title: Sting vs. Nikita Koloff. Rick Rude vs. Rick Steiner. Brian
Pillman vs. Jushin Liger. Paul Orndorff vs. Junk Yard Dog. Arn Anderson
vs. Larry Zbyszko. Cactus Jack vs. Big Van Vader. And much more!
