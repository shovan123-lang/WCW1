KOLOFF AND LUGER: NO WINNER IN INGLEWOOD

The World Heavyweight Title rematch tour hit the West Coast, and for the
second straight night it ended outside the ring. Nikita Koloff and Lex
Luger traded clotheslines for nearly seventeen minutes while Harley
Race and Mr. Hughes worked the referee from the floor. When the fight
spilled over the rail, neither man made it back, and the referee
counted them both out at 16:56. Koloff keeps the title. The two kept
brawling up the aisle and into the back. Oakland is Friday.

WINDHAM AND STEINER TAKE DOWN RUDE'S TEAM

Barry Windham and Rick Steiner beat new United States Champion Rick
Rude and Bobby Eaton when Windham hauled Eaton off the top rope with a
superplex at 12:21. Paul E. Dangerously tried to swing it, going after
Steiner on the floor, but Windham got the three. Rude wasn't finished.
He jumped Windham after the bell and laid him out with the Rude
Awakening, holding the U.S. belt over him before Paul E. pulled him to
the back.

PILLMAN WINS THE SERIES

Brian Pillman and Jushin Liger had split two matches. In Inglewood,
in Liger's last WCW match before he goes home to New Japan, Pillman
settled it, catching him with a springboard clothesline at 20:03. They
shook hands in the middle of the ring, and the Forum gave them both a
standing ovation.

FREEBIRDS KEEP THE GOLD

With PN News out with a torn quad, Tracy Smothers teamed with the Junk
Yard Dog against the World Tag Team Champions. Michael Hayes caught JYD
with the DDT at 12:42, and the Fabulous Freebirds are still the
champions. Smothers and the Young Pistols get their own shot at the
belts in Oakland on Friday.

ZENK STAYS UNBEATEN

Butch Reed's travel ran late and he didn't make it to the Forum, so
his partner Dick Murdoch stepped in against Tom Zenk. Murdoch roughed
him up for most of thirteen minutes, but Zenk caught him with a dropkick
off the second rope and pinned him at 13:08. Zenk is still unbeaten in
WCW.

AROUND THE RING

Tommy Rich pinned Diamond Dallas Page with a bodypress at 8:55. Big Van
Vader flattened Robert Gibson with the Vader Bomb at 6:05. Paul
Orndorff piledrove Ozz at 10:21. The York Foundation opened the show
when Richard Morton rolled up Jim Brunzell at 12:15.
