THE NEW WORLD HEAVYWEIGHT CHAMPION
Baltimore, Baltimore Arena | Segment 17 (4:00)

The referee's hand hits the mat for the three. Lex Luger rolls off Barry
Windham, still on his knees, staring at the steel chair lying next to
him. The bell rings over and over.

JIM ROSS: It's over. Lex Luger is the World Heavyweight Champion. But
Tony, you saw it. Everybody in this building saw it. That chair came
through the door.

TONY SCHIAVONE: Harley Race had the referee tied up and Mr. Hughes slid
that chair in, Jim. They said nobody could get in. They found a way.

The door is unlocked. Harley Race and Mr. Hughes walk in. Hughes kicks
the chair out through the door. Race hugs Luger around the shoulders.
Then Jim Herd steps through the door in a suit, carrying the new World
Heavyweight Championship belt. The crowd boos as he hands it to Luger.

Luger stands, holds the belt up over his head, then pulls it down and
looks at it for a long moment. He climbs to the second rope and raises it
toward the hard camera. Race hands him a microphone.

LEX LUGER: I told you. I told every single one of you. Three years of
next time. Three years of almost. Look at it. Look at it! The Total
Package is the World Heavyweight Champion, and nobody, nobody in this
company is ever going to take it away from me.

Behind him, Barry Windham grabs the cage and pulls himself up to his
feet. The crowd sees him first and starts to cheer. Luger turns around.
The two of them stare at each other across the ring. Windham points at
the belt, then at Luger. Hughes steps in front of the champion, and Race
pulls Luger out through the door.

JIM ROSS: Lex Luger is the champion, but you'd better believe Barry
Windham is not finished with him. Not by a long shot.

Luger backs up the aisle with Race and Hughes, the belt held high, while
Windham stands alone in the cage with the fans chanting his name.
