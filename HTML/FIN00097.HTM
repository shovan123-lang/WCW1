OPEN: JR & TONY - LUGER'S FIRST WEEK AS CHAMPION

Jim Ross and Tony Schiavone welcome viewers to Hara Arena in Dayton, Ohio.
Ross calls it the first week of a new era in WCW. Three nights ago in
Baltimore, inside a steel cage, Lex Luger piledrove Barry Windham and won
the vacant World Heavyweight Championship. Tony notes that Luger did it
clean. Ross answers that what Luger did afterward wasn't clean at all: he
put Harley Race and Mr. Hughes in his corner.

Tony runs down the card. Ron Simmons meets Arn Anderson. Brian Pillman
gets Bobby Eaton with Paul E. Dangerously at ringside. In the main event,
Barry Windham, still smarting from the cage, faces Mr. Hughes. Ross says
Windham has wanted a piece of Luger since Baltimore, and tonight the
champion's bodyguard stands in his way.

Ross closes: "The champion is here tonight, and so is Harley Race. Folks,
something tells me the man who wears the gold won't stay in the back."
