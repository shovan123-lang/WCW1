Match observations: 

Great American Bash Tour | July 4th, 1991
Wildwood, NJ |Venue: Wildwood Convention Center
Attendance: 5,500 AVG: Ticket $15
Gate: $$82,500 Broadcast: House Show

Wildwood delivered a solid mid tour show on this 4th of July holiday! 
The show was anchored by a strong tag bout from Doom, a surprisingly hot 
Cactus-Vader confrontation, and a main event that gave Sting a gritty, heroic
defense. The crowd wasn't as loud as Macon, but they reacted big to Sting, Doom
and Cactus Jack.

Young Pistols vs Freebirds:
Pistols continue to gain momentum heading into the tag title picture. Freebirds
were over but clearly slowing down. Pistols' win helps establish them as the
"young guns" of the division. 

Bobby Eaton vs Big Josh
Eaton needed a decisive win after recent losses. Quick match, clean Alabama
Jam, keeps him strong for Syndicate six-man tags. 

DDP vs PN News
This was about getting Page heat. Kimberly's interference worked, though 
the match went long for the rating it produced. PN News stays sympathetic;
Page gets a meaningful win. 

Doom vs Killer Bees
The fans picked this one as the best match of the night. The Bees gave Doom
a long, competitive bout, but Simmons' powerslam finish keeps Doom looking
Dominant. This also subtly foreshadows the Reed/Simmons split-Reed was visibly
frustrated post-match. 

Cactus Jack vs Vader
Harley Race's interference protects both men. Cactus took a beating but
stayed standing, which helps build him as a dangerous wild card. Vader remains
the monster heel of the tour. 

Nikita Koloff vs Larry Zbyszko
Paul E's interference keeps the dangerous alliance-adjacent heat alive.
Nikita stays strong without beating Zbyszko clean. This can feed into 
a Nikita vs Paul E. Syndicate program.

Sting vs Abdullah The Butcher- U.S. Title Match.
On the Fourth of July, it was only fitting that the United States 
championship would be defended in our main evemt. 
The long, bloody-feeling brawl without actual blood. Sting making Abby
pass out in the Scorpion Deathlock is a huge babyface moment. Abby is
leaving for All Japan, so this was the right way to write him out stong,
but decisively. 

Backstage Notes: 
-  Sting remains the top draw- crowd was hottest for him by far.
- Doom and Cactus Jack are gaining momentum with live audiences. 
- PN News continues to get reactions despite mixed match quality.
- York Foundation interference angles are working, Morton/Taylor/Rich
are drawing heat. 

There has been some frustration backstage with guys like Abdullah and Vader
making commitments overseas. With the departure of Ric Flair, guys like
Abdullah and Vader are needed now more than ever. Some are expecting to
see their availability to these other promotions start to slow down in
the coming months.
