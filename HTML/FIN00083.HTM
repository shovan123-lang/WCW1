"YOU'RE NEXT": RICK RUDE'S MESSAGE
Baltimore, Baltimore Arena | Segment 13 (2:00)

The referee raises Rick Rude's hand. Rick Steiner is still flat on his
back, holding his jaw where the phone caught him. Paul E. Dangerously
hands Rude a microphone, and Rude swivels his hips as the boos pour down.

RICK RUDE: Steiner, that's what happens when a college boy tries to hang
with a Ravishing man. And Dustin Rhodes, wherever you're sitting
tonight, I hope that neck brace is comfortable.

Rude steps over Steiner and walks to the center of the ring. He looks
straight into the hard camera.

RICK RUDE: But I didn't fly halfway around the world to beat up the
undercard. Sting! I know you're back there, getting ready for Nikita
Koloff. Enjoy him. Enjoy that pretty little belt while you've got it.
Because when you're done with the Russian, you've got a Ravishing
problem. Sting, you're next.

Paul E. holds his phone out toward the camera like he's dialing Sting's
number, then snaps it shut. Steiner starts to stir, and the two of them
slide out under the bottom rope and back up the aisle, Rude still
swiveling his hips.

JIM ROSS: Rick Rude has just put the United States Champion on notice,
and Sting hasn't even wrestled yet tonight.
