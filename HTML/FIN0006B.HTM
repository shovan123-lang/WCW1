WCW SATURDAY NIGHT OPEN: ONE NIGHT TO BALTIMORE
Greenville, Memorial Auditorium | Segment 1 (2:00)

The show opens on a wide shot of a packed Memorial Auditorium. Fans hold
up signs for Sting, Luger and Windham. The camera finds Jim Ross and Tony
Schiavone at ringside.

JIM ROSS: Good evening, everybody, and welcome to WCW Saturday Night. I'm
Jim Ross, alongside Tony Schiavone, and we are in Greenville, South
Carolina, less than twenty-four hours from the Great American Bash.

TONY SCHIAVONE: Jim, tomorrow night in Baltimore, the World Heavyweight
Championship will be decided inside a steel cage. Lex Luger and Barry
Windham. One cage, one belt, and no way out.

JIM ROSS: And that's just the top of the card. Sting defends the United
States Title against Nikita Koloff. Ravishing Rick Rude meets Rick
Steiner. Brian Pillman and Jushin Liger run it back. Paul Orndorff and
the Junkyard Dog. Arn Anderson and Larry Zbyszko finally settle it. El
Gigante and the One Man Gang. Cactus Jack against Big Van Vader. It is
the deepest card this company has put together in years.

TONY SCHIAVONE: And tonight, a lot of those men get one last chance to
say their piece before the cage door closes. Let's get started.
