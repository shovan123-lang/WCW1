Match observations: 

Great American Bash Tour| July 1st, 1991
Day 1: Macon, Georgia|Venue:Macon Coliseum
 Attendance: 5,400 |AVG Ticket Price: $15.00 
Gate $81,000| Broadcast: House Show

WCW's July 1st, 1991 event at the Macon Coliseum delivered a mix of gritty 
southern brawls, rising star showcases, and a marathon main event that 
tested endurance and storytelling. PN News opened with a surprising DQ 
win over Arn Anderson after Paul E. Dangerously’s interference, while 
One Man Gang flattened Big Josh with the 747 Splash in a brief slugfest. 

The standout tag bout saw The Brass Knuckle Syndicate—Dick Murdoch and Bobby 
Eaton—score a hard-fought victory over The Killer Bees in nearly 20 minutes 
of stiff,old-school action. Richard Morton continued his York Foundation 
momentum with a clean win over Dustin Rhodes in a technically sound, 
emotionally charged match. The mid-card featured Rick Steiner overpowering 
Ozz, and The Fabulous Freebirds edging out The Young Pistols in a fast-paced
southern tag. 
Barry Windham and Nikita Koloff clashed in a physical bout that ended with
Windham’s lariat sealing the win. The night’s centerpiece was a grueling 
Iron Man Match where Lex Luger and Sting narrowly outlasted Big Van Vader 
and Cactus Jack, 5 falls to 4,despite interference from Harley Race. 
Sting’s back-to-back flying crossbody pins on Vader turned the tide, 
showcasing his resilience and crowd connection. 

Overall, the Macon crowd saw a monster night of action to kick off the Great
American Bash tour! 

The train rolls on to Ft. Bragg, NC tomorrow night.
