Match observations: 

WCW MAGAZINE: GREAT AMERICAN BASH TOUR
Farmland, Indiana | Monroe Central High School Gym | July 16, 1991

STING'S GOLD SHINES IN THE HEARTLAND

There were 600 fans packed into the bleachers at Monroe Central High
School, and every one of them came to see the United States Champion. Sting
didn't let them down, but Bill Irwin made him work for every second of it.

The Wild Thing took the champion to the floor, rammed him into the gym's
folding bleachers and kept him grounded for the better part of fifteen
minutes. Irwin went for the title like it was his ticket out of the
preliminaries, and for a while it looked like it might be. Then Sting
caught him coming off the ropes, climbed to the top turnbuckle and came
off with a diving cross body for the three count at 18:44. Sting is still
the United States Champion, and the kids in Farmland went home hoarse.

ZENK 2, PAUL E. 0

Paul E. Dangerously has spent two nights trying to buy Tom Zenk and two
nights watching it backfire. This time he didn't wait. Barely a minute
into Zenk's rematch with Bobby Eaton, Paul E. climbed onto the apron with
his portable phone and swung it at the Z-Man in full view of the referee.
The bell rang at 1:40. Zenk wins by disqualification and leads the series
two to nothing, and Paul E. left the gym arguing with his own wrestler.

UPSET OF THE TOUR?

The Bruise Brothers have flattened everybody WCW has put in front of them
since Philadelphia. Not in Farmland. Robert Gibson and PN News took
everything Ron and Don Harris had, and when Don went for one big boot too
many, News climbed to the second rope and came down with a cannonball
senton for the pin at 13:32. The Harris twins stared at each other in the
ring for a long time afterward. Neither one said a word.

PILLMAN PUTS RICH TO SLEEP

Flyin' Brian showed a mean streak in a grueling 14-minute battle with
Tommy Rich, locking on a sleeperhold in the middle of the ring and refusing
to let go until the referee called it. Rich, who had the crowd behind him
from the opening bell, never quit. He just ran out of air.

ALSO IN FARMLAND

Nikita Koloff stepped in for the injured Big Josh and flattened Black
Bart with the Russian Sickle at 8:33. And Tracy Smothers got one back for
the Young Pistols, rolling up Terry Taylor with a small package at 7:07.
