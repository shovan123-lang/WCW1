Match observations: 

Match observations:

Knoxville Civic Coliseum
Knoxville, TN
July 21, 1991
Attendance: 3,300
Average ticket: $10.00
Gate: $33,000
Merchandise: $6,600

VADER TAKES THE COUNT-OUT, BUT WINDHAM TAKES KNOXVILLE

Knoxville bought tickets to see Sting defend the U.S. Title against Big
Van Vader. Sting is gone and the title belongs to Rick Rude, who was on
the other side of the world tonight. That left Barry Windham, on his
first night in Sting's spot, to stand in with a 400-pound monster. He
didn't back down for one second.

WINDHAM'S FIRST NIGHT

For nineteen minutes Windham and Vader beat on each other, chop for chop,
and the Coliseum was on Windham's side the whole way. Windham kicked Vader
out of the ring, and the big man barely made it back in before ten.
Moments later Vader returned the favor, and this time Windham didn't
make it. Referee Jimmy Jett counted him out at 19:06. Vader gets the win,
but nobody went home thinking he'd beaten Windham. If this is how Windham
fills Sting's dates, WCW will be fine.

THE FREEBIRDS' FIRST DEFENSE

One night after winning the tag tournament in Johnson City, Michael Hayes
and Jimmy Garvin defended the WCW World Tag Team Titles against the Killer
Bees. The crowd was with the Bees, and Jim Brunzell had Hayes in trouble
with a crucifix. Hayes got out of it and dropped Brunzell with the DDT for
the pin at 10:07. Diamond Dallas Page didn't have to lift a finger. The
Freebirds are still champions.

ZENK BEATS THE PHONE

Paul E. Dangerously tried everything to get Bobby Eaton a win over Tom
Zenk. He slid Eaton the telephone. Then he climbed in himself and hit Zenk
with it while the referee was turned away. Zenk still found a way. He
caught Eaton with the flying cross body for the pin at 13:38. That's two
singles wins over Eaton this month, and Paul E. is running out of ideas.

LIGER FLIES IN TENNESSEE

Jushin Liger beat Tommy Rich in their first meeting. Rich gave him all he
could handle with a flying bulldog and a front suplex, but Liger came off
the apron with the Asai moonsault and pinned him at 10:35.

TAYLOR AND SMOTHERS STEAL ONE

With PN News out after tearing his quad in Johnson City, Tracy Smothers
stepped in against Terry Taylor and nearly stole the night. Smothers hit
the Southern Kick and had the crowd standing, but Taylor caught him with
the Five Arm at 12:09. It was one of the best matches on the card.

AROUND THE RING

Dick Murdoch beat Robert Gibson with a brainbuster at 8:29. In the opener,
Steve Armstrong pinned Black Bart with a missile dropkick at 10:10.
