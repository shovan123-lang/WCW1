Match observations: 

Civic Center
Charleston, WV
July 19, 1991
Attendance: 8,250
Average ticket: $13.00
Gate: $107,250
Merchandise: $20,265

RUDE ENDS STING'S REIGN! RAVISHING RICK IS U.S. CHAMPION

Sting came to Charleston for what was his last scheduled WCW date for the
foreseeable future, and he left without the United States Championship.
After a brutal 27:07, the longest match of the night by a mile, Rick Rude
caught the champion with a vertical facebuster and covered him for the
three count. Rick Rude is the new United States Champion.

It was a war from the opening bell. Rude worked over Sting's back and
neck, Sting fired back with the Stinger splash and twice had Rude in
trouble, and the 8,250 fans in the Civic Center were on their feet for the
last ten minutes. Then Paul E. Dangerously got involved. With the
referee's back turned, Dangerously went after Sting from the floor, and
seconds later Rude had him up for the facebuster. Sting sat in the ring
long after the bell, staring at the empty spot on his waist, as Rude and
Dangerously celebrated up the aisle.

KOLOFF KEEPS THE GOLD, BARELY

One night after winning the World Heavyweight Championship, Nikita Koloff
gave Lex Luger his rematch, and it didn't take long for Luger's corner to
get involved. Harley Race and Mr. Hughes both went after Koloff, and at
15:23 the referee had seen enough and threw the match out. Koloff wins by
disqualification and is still the champion. Luger can say he wasn't
beaten, but he doesn't have the belt.

ZENK AND PILLMAN WIN AGAIN

Brian Pillman and Tom Zenk teamed up for the best tag match of the night
and beat Bobby Eaton and Larry Zbyszko in 15:06. Zenk pinned Zbyszko with
a T-bone suplex. That's four straight wins for Zenk since he arrived in
WCW.

ZBYSZKO'S BAD NIGHT GETS WORSE

Zbyszko wasn't done for the night. When Rick Steiner had Arn Anderson in
trouble, Zbyszko ran in and attacked Steiner. The referee disqualified
Anderson at 10:17. Steiner gets the win, but he'll want to know why
Zbyszko is suddenly doing Arn Anderson's dirty work.

MR. WONDERFUL STILL UNBEATEN

Paul Orndorff kept his perfect summer going, putting Tommy Rich away with
the piledriver at 10:16. Twenty-four hours after he pinned Sting in
Cleveland, Orndorff is starting to look like the man to beat in the
Television Title tournament.

THE GIANT WINS THE BATTLE

El Gigante and One Man Gang have been staring each other down since
Dayton. Tonight they finally collided, and the Giant came out on top,
pinning the Gang with a chokeslam at 5:21.

AROUND THE RING

Ron Simmons flattened Diamond Dallas Page with a spinebuster at 13:20.
The night before the tag team title tournament, the Young Pistols got off
to a flying start in the opener when Steve Armstrong pinned Butch Reed
with a flying clothesline at 8:10, beating Reed and Dick Murdoch. Junk Yard
Dog beat Bill Irwin with the Thump powerslam at 10:07.
