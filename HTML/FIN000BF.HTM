CACTUS JACK TO THE BRUISE BROTHERS
Johnson City, TN (taped) | Segment 2 (2:00)

A dark room lit by candles. Cactus Jack sits in a folding chair in a
back brace. The One Man Gang stands behind him, holding a candle.

CACTUS JACK: Ron. Don. Harris boys. You had a title shot tonight, and
Michael Hayes rolled you up like a sleeping bag. Two of the biggest,
meanest men in this building, beaten by a victory roll. You know why?
Because you fight alone. Two of you, and nobody watching your back.

The Gang knows what that's like. Many nights he walks to
the ring by himself, and he walks back to the dressing room by
himself. Didn't you, Gang?

The Gang lowers the candle and says nothing.

CACTUS JACK: I can't wrestle right now. Vader saw to that, and I saw to
Vader. But I don't need to wrestle to see what's coming. The Dark
Circle doesn't lose, because the Dark Circle doesn't fight alone. Think
about it, boys. Think hard. Bang bang.

Cactus blows out the candle. Black.
