THE LAST WORD FROM HARLEY RACE
Greenville, Memorial Auditorium | Segment 6 (2:00)

Harley Race walks onto the interview set, with Mr. Hughes looming behind
him in his sunglasses. Tony Schiavone holds out the microphone.

TONY SCHIAVONE: Harley, you've been in Lex Luger's corner, and you've
been in the ring yourself all summer. What happens in Baltimore?

HARLEY RACE: Schiavone, I've held the World Title eight times. I've
wrestled in every building, every country and every town worth
wrestling in. There's nothing left out there for me to prove with my
hands. So starting tomorrow night in Baltimore, Harley Race is done
wrestling. From now on, I'm in one corner and one corner only. Lex
Luger's.

HARLEY RACE: And Mr. Hughes goes where Lex Luger goes. He's not with the
Dark Circle anymore. He's not sitting around waiting on Kevin Sullivan
to come back from wherever he ran off to. He's protecting the next World
Champion.

Hughes takes off his sunglasses and stares into the camera.

HARLEY RACE: Barry Windham, you asked for a cage so nobody could get in.
You forgot something, son. Nobody can get you out, either. Tomorrow
night Lex Luger becomes the World Champion, and I'll be at ringside to
watch it happen.

Race slaps Hughes on the chest, and the two of them walk off the set.
