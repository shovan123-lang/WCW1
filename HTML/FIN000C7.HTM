THE NEW CHAMPIONS / PAUL E. FINDS ARN AND ZBYSZKO / CLOSE
Johnson City, TN | Segment 6 (5:00)

In the ring. The referee hands the belts to Michael Hayes and Jimmy
Garvin. "Badstreet U.S.A." hits. Hayes struts. Garvin drapes his belt
over Diamond Dallas Page's shoulder for a second, then takes it back.
Hayes grabs the mic.

MICHAEL HAYES: Johnson City! You have just seen history! The Fabulous
Freebirds, the greatest tag team that ever lived, are the FIRST WCW
World Tag Team Champions! And there is nobody, NOBODY, in that locker
room who's gonna take them off us! Badstreet!

On the floor, the trainer checks on Tracy Smothers. Smothers and Steve
Armstrong stare up at the ring.

Backstage. A hallway. Arn Anderson sits on an equipment case taping his
wrist. Larry Zbyszko leans on the wall across from him. Neither looks
at the other. Paul E. walks in on the phone and hangs up.

PAUL E. DANGEROUSLY: Gentlemen. Did you see that? The Freebirds.
Champions. You know why they're champions? Because you two were too
busy fighting each other to notice the belts were up for grabs.

Arn keeps taping.

PAUL E. DANGEROUSLY: Arn, you're the best technician in this company.
Larry, nobody's smarter in a ring. Last night in Charleston you already
proved you're on the same side. Together, with me as your manager,
you'd be the best team in this company. Not the second best. The best.

ARN ANDERSON: (still taping) I'm listening.

LARRY ZBYSZKO: (pushes off the wall) Paul, I've heard a lot of pitches.
Before I hear another word... what's my cut?

Paul E. smiles and holds up the phone.

PAUL E. DANGEROUSLY: Larry, that's the right question.

Back to the desk. JR and Tony.

JIM ROSS: What a night in Johnson City. Nikita Koloff says this is his
last ride. Barry Windham says he's taking every one of Sting's dates.
The Freebirds are the first WCW World Tag Team Champions. And Paul E.
Dangerously is making somebody an offer.

TONY SCHIAVONE: And some bad news, JR. PN News was helped to the back
after the Vader Bomb. He's injured his left leg, and we're told he'll
be out for some time.

JIM ROSS: Vader is hurting people, Tony. Tomorrow night in Knoxville,
he gets Barry Windham. And the Freebirds defend those belts for the
first time against the Killer Bees.

TONY SCHIAVONE: Monday in Nashville, Koloff defends against Luger.
Tuesday in Inglewood, Windham and Steiner against Rude and Eaton. And
next Saturday, Saturday Night comes to Phoenix, Arizona: Koloff and El
Gigante against Vader and the One Man Gang, and Windham and Simmons
against Luger and Hughes.

JIM ROSS: Every town, every night. For Tony Schiavone, I'm Jim Ross.
Good night from Johnson City!
