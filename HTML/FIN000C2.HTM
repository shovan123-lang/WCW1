PAUL E. DANGEROUSLY AND BOBBY EATON / RICK RUDE ON TAPE
Johnson City, TN | Segment 3 (3:00)

The interview area. Eric Bischoff with a mic. Paul E. holds the
cellular phone. Bobby Eaton stands behind him.

ERIC BISCHOFF: Paul E., last night in Charleston your client Rick Rude
won the United States Championship...

PAUL E. DANGEROUSLY: Won it? WON it? He took it, Bischoff. Sting gave
him everything he had, and Rick Rude gave it back with interest. And
now Sting is gone, because he knows there's no rematch he can win.

ERIC BISCHOFF: There are people who say you got involved.

PAUL E. DANGEROUSLY: There are people who say a lot of things. They
don't have the belt. My client does. And my client is away tonight on business,
so he left all of you a message. Roll it.

Taped in Charleston, WV, after the show 7/19. Rude in his robe, the
U.S. belt over his shoulder, a cinder-block wall behind him.

RICK RUDE: Johnson City. While you're watching this, I'm on my way to
Japan with the United States Championship in my carry-on. Sting? I beat
you. And when it was over, you were sitting on the mat looking at the
empty spot on your waist.

And let me clear something up. Paul E. didn't win me this belt. I saw
an opening and I took it. That's the difference between Ravishing Rick
Rude and every sweat hog in this company. I don't miss.

So, ladies, take a good long look, because this is what a United
States Champion looks like. And fellas...

He turns and shows the belt across his back.

RICK RUDE: ...this is what you'll never have.

Back to live. Paul E. laughs. Eaton smirks.

PAUL E. DANGEROUSLY: That's my client. And Bobby Eaton is my
investment. Every man in my portfolio pays dividends. Somebody in that
locker room is about to learn that the hard way.

Paul E. taps the phone against his palm. They walk off.
