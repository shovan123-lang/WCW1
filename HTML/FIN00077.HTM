THE GREAT AMERICAN BASH '91: OPENING
Baltimore, Baltimore Arena | Segment 1 (3:00)

Fireworks go off over the entrance stage as the pay-per-view comes on
the air. The camera pans across a packed Baltimore Arena, then up to the
steel cage hanging from the rafters above the ring. Jim Ross and Tony
Schiavone are at the broadcast table.

JIM ROSS: Hello, everybody, and welcome to the Great American Bash, live
from the Baltimore Arena! I'm Jim Ross, alongside Tony Schiavone, and
take a look above the ring. That steel cage comes down tonight, and when
it goes back up, World Championship Wrestling will have a new World
Heavyweight Champion.

TONY SCHIAVONE: Lex Luger or Barry Windham, Jim. For the first time since
Ric Flair walked out of this company, somebody is going to wear that
belt.

JIM ROSS: But before we go any further, we have to talk about what
happened last night in Greenville. Dustin Rhodes was attacked by Paul E.
Dangerously, Rick Rude and Bobby Eaton, and he left that building on a
stretcher. Tony, what's the latest?

TONY SCHIAVONE: Dustin spent the night in the hospital in Greenville and
was released this morning, Jim. He's home in a neck brace. The doctors
say there's no permanent damage, but he won't wrestle again until the
fall. And Rick Steiner, who saw the whole thing from the aisle, gets
Rick Rude tonight.

JIM ROSS: Sting defends the United States Title against Nikita Koloff.
Pillman and Liger. Cactus Jack and Big Van Vader. Arn and Larry. It is
all here tonight, folks, and it all starts right now.
