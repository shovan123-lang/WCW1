THE Z-MAN SAYS NO: TOM ZENK'S WCW DEBUT
Baltimore, Baltimore Arena | Segment 5 (5:00)

The lights go down after the Morton vs. Gibson match. Paul E. Dangerously
walks out with his portable phone to his ear, with Bobby Eaton a step
behind him. They head straight to the ring. Paul E. calls for a
microphone.

JIM ROSS: Well, here comes trouble. Paul E. Dangerously and Bobby Eaton,
and Eaton isn't even on this card tonight.

TONY SCHIAVONE: Paul E. has had his hand in just about every match on this
tour, Jim. Whatever he wants, it can't be good.

PAUL E. DANGEROUSLY: Baltimore! Shut up and listen, because this is the
most important announcement you will hear all night. Everybody in this
building came to see who walks out of that cage with the World Title.
Fine. But I am thinking about next year, and the year after that, because
that's what a genius does.

The crowd boos. Paul E. waves them off.

PAUL E. DANGEROUSLY: Three days ago, World Championship Wrestling signed a
young man with more upside than anybody on this roster. Good looks,
athletic ability, a future. And the minute I heard about it, I picked up
this phone and I made him an offer. Because talent like that should not
be wasted. It should be managed. By me. So it gives me
great pleasure to introduce the newest investment of Paul E.
Dangerously: the Z-Man, Tom Zenk!

Zenk's music hits. Tom Zenk comes through the curtain to a big reaction,
slapping hands on the way down the aisle. He climbs in. Paul E. holds out
a hand. Zenk doesn't take it. He takes the microphone instead.

TOM ZENK: Paul, I appreciate the phone calls. All eleven of them.

The crowd laughs.

TOM ZENK: And I read the contract you sent over. It was very generous.

Zenk pulls a folded contract out of his jacket, holds it up, and tears it
in half. The crowd pops.

TOM ZENK: But I didn't come to WCW to carry your phone. I signed with WCW
to take on the best wrestlers in the world, one at a time, on my own. And
if that means starting with whoever you send after me, that's fine with
me.

Paul E.'s smile drops. He looks at Eaton. Zenk turns to face Paul E., and
Eaton jumps him from behind with a forearm across the back of the neck.

JIM ROSS: Oh, come on! From behind! Eaton jumped him from behind!

Eaton stomps away at Zenk in the corner while Paul E. shouts instructions.
Eaton climbs to the top rope, looking for the Alabama Jam. Zenk rolls out
of the way, Eaton crashes, and Zenk is back up. He catches Eaton coming
off the ropes with a dropkick that sends him through the ropes to the
floor.

TONY SCHIAVONE: What a dropkick! The Z-Man can go, Jim!

Paul E. bails out of the ring and drags Eaton up the aisle by the arm,
yelling over his shoulder. Zenk stands in the middle of the ring with the
two halves of the contract and throws them toward the aisle.

JIM ROSS: Paul E. Dangerously thought he was buying a new toy tonight. He
just bought himself a problem. Tom Zenk is here, he's his own man, and
something tells me Bobby Eaton is going to get his chance to back up that
cheap shot.

Paul E. and Eaton watch from the top of the ramp as Zenk raises a fist to
the crowd. The camera cuts back to the announcers.
