KOLOFF CHOKES OUT LUGER! THE RUSSIAN NIGHTMARE IS WORLD CHAMPION

Four days. That's how long Lex Luger's reign as World Heavyweight Champion
lasted. In front of 5,200 fans at the Convention Center in Cleveland,
Nikita Koloff locked a front facelock on the champion in the middle of the
ring, and at 23:17 Luger went limp. The referee lifted his arm three times.
It didn't come up. Nikita Koloff is the new World Heavyweight Champion.

THE SHOT HE WAS OWED

Koloff came to Cleveland with a lot to answer for. Luger cradled him in
Philadelphia two weeks ago, and Sting made him submit at the Great American
Bash. Tonight he wrestled like a man who knew he might not get another
chance. For twenty minutes the two powerhouses traded bearhugs,
clotheslines and slams, and twice Luger had the piledriver set up before
Koloff backdropped his way out.

Harley Race and Mr. Hughes were in the champion's corner, as they have been
all week. But when Nikita cinched the facelock, Luger's arms flailed toward
the ropes and never found them, and by the time Race realized what was
happening the bell had rung. Luger was still out on the canvas as Koloff
held the belt over his head.

MR. WONDERFUL TOPPLES STING

Paul Orndorff has said all summer that nobody in WCW can beat him. Tonight
he put his unbeaten record on the line against the United States Champion
himself, and he kept it. After nearly sixteen minutes of the
hardest-hitting action of the night, Orndorff caught Sting coming off the
ropes and drove him into the mat with the piledriver. One, two, three at
15:48. The title wasn't on the line, but Sting will want this one back.

BADSTREET IS READY

Two nights before the tag team title tournament in Johnson City, the
Fabulous Freebirds sent a message. Michael Hayes and Jimmy Garvin outlasted
the Killer Bees in a fast, rough 14:38, and Hayes put Jim Brunzell away
with the DDT. Diamond Dallas Page was at ringside, grinning.

MUTA'S MIST

Rick Steiner had the Great Muta reeling when Muta turned and sprayed the
green mist into his eyes. The referee disqualified Muta at 10:04, but
Steiner was the one left rubbing his face in the corner.

AROUND THE RING

Jushin Liger flew over the top with a breathtaking Asai moonsault to pin
Richard Morton at 12:06. Tom Zenk is still unbeaten in WCW. He beat Diamond
Dallas Page at 8:39 even after Kimberly got involved. Junk Yard Dog
flattened Butch Reed with the Thump powerslam at 7:19. In the opener,
Robert Gibson and PN News beat Bill Irwin and Black Bart when Gibson caught
Bart with a superkick at 12:51.
