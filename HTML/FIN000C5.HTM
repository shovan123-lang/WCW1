SIMMONS VS. HUGHES: POST-MATCH ANGLE
Johnson City, TN | Segment 5 (after the match, 1:30)

Lex Luger slid in and clubbed Ron Simmons from behind for the
disqualification. After the bell, Luger and Mr. Hughes double-team
Simmons in the corner while Harley Race puts the boots to him.

Barry Windham hits the ring, still in his street clothes, and goes
straight for Hughes. He clotheslines Hughes over the top rope to the
floor. Luger turns around into a right hand and rolls out under the
bottom rope. Race pulls Luger back up the aisle.

Windham grabs a mic.

BARRY WINDHAM: Luger! You want to jump people from behind? Next
Saturday. Phoenix, Arizona. Me and Ron Simmons, against you and your
bodyguard.

Race grabs the ring announcer's mic on the floor.

HARLEY RACE: You want it, Windham? You got it. Phoenix. And bring a
stretcher.

Windham helps Simmons to his feet. They stand together in the ring.

JIM ROSS: Barry Windham told us he was done watching, and he meant it.

TONY SCHIAVONE: Windham and Simmons against Luger and Hughes. Next
week, Saturday Night, Phoenix.
