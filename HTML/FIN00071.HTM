INVESTMENT RETURNS: THE ATTACK ON DUSTIN RHODES
Greenville, Memorial Auditorium | Segment 7 (5:00)

Tony Schiavone stands in the ring. Dustin Rhodes comes down the aisle in
jeans and a WCW T-shirt, wearing a stiff foam neck brace. The fans give
him a warm welcome, and he climbs through the ropes slowly.

TONY SCHIAVONE: Ladies and gentlemen, Dustin Rhodes. Dustin, you were
supposed to be in the six-man tag tomorrow night in Baltimore. Tell
these people what happened.

DUSTIN RHODES: Tony, Columbus, Georgia, last Monday. I went twenty minutes
with Jushin Liger, the best twenty minutes I've ever had. Somewhere in
there I came down wrong on my neck. I finished the match. Didn't feel it
till the next morning, when I couldn't turn my head.

DUSTIN RHODES: The doctors say I'm out until the fall. That's the hardest
thing anybody's ever told me. I wanted to stand in that ring in
Baltimore with the Young Pistols more than anything in the world. So I
came down here tonight to tell these people I'm sorry, and to tell Big
Josh to go get 'em for me.

The crowd cheers. Then the boos start. Paul E. Dangerously walks out onto
the stage holding up his portable phone, with Ravishing Rick Rude and
Bobby Eaton on either side of him.

JIM ROSS: Oh, no. Not now. Not tonight.

PAUL E. DANGEROUSLY: Dustin! Dustin, Dustin, Dustin. What a touching
story. Really, I'm all choked up. But you left something out, kid. You
think that neck just went out by itself? Richmond, Virginia. Rick Rude
had you for seventeen minutes, and every one of them was on your neck.
Liger just finished the job.

The three of them start down the aisle as Paul E. keeps talking.

PAUL E. DANGEROUSLY: This morning on WCW Pro, I told the whole world I
make investments. Well, every smart investor protects his investment.
And Rick Rude doesn't need a Rhodes in his way this fall. So tonight,
we close the account.

Schiavone backs out of the ring. Dustin doesn't. He stands in the middle
of the ring and waves them in.

JIM ROSS: Dustin, get out of there! He can't defend himself!

Rude stops on the apron and takes the microphone from Paul E.

RICK RUDE: Before I do this, I want all you sweaty, out-of-shape
Greenville fans to keep the noise down, so the ladies can watch a real
man finish what he started.

Rude and Eaton step through the ropes. Dustin fires away with left hands
and backs Rude into the corner, but Eaton comes in behind him with a
forearm across the back of the neck. Dustin goes down to a knee. Eaton
hooks both of his arms from behind and holds him up.

Paul E. climbs in, winds up and smashes the portable phone across
Dustin's jaw. Rude rips off the neck brace and throws it into the crowd.
He grabs Dustin's head, swivels his hips for the fans, and drops him with
the Rude Awakening, driving the injured neck into the mat.

JIM ROSS: That's his neck! That's his injured neck! Somebody get out here!

TONY SCHIAVONE: He's not moving, Jim. Dustin's not moving.

Rude stands over Dustin and swivels his hips again. Paul E. holds the
phone to his ear and pretends to make a call.

PAUL E. DANGEROUSLY: Yeah. Yeah, it's done. Put it on my tab.
